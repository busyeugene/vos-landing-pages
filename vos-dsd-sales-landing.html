Sound Familiar?

The order gets written at the stop. The product is on the shelf back home.

On a DSD run, the order and the load get decided hours apart. When the first one is a guess, the second one is wrong:

🚚

"We loaded for last week"

Nobody knew what the stores wanted, so the truck went out on an average and came back carrying half of it.

📝

"It is on a pad in the cab"

Handwritten at the counter, keyed in that evening if the driver remembers. One digit out and the next delivery is short.

⏰

"The manager was not in"

Whoever can say what to order is off shift when the driver calls, so you get whatever the closer guessed at.

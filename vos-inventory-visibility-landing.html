Sound Familiar?

Everyone is working from a different stock number.

The stock count exists. The problem is who can actually see it, and how old it is by the time they do. Here's what that looks like in a real week:

📵

"Let me check with the warehouse and call you back"

A customer asks your rep if an item is in. The rep phones the warehouse, waits while someone walks the aisle, then calls the customer back. By then the answer may have changed.

📧

"The stock sheet went out this morning"

Someone exports a stock list first thing and sends it to the reps. By noon, orders have eaten into it, and the reps are still quoting from the morning copy.

🚫

"They ordered it, and we didn't have it"

Your customer had no way to check, so they ordered an item you were out of. They found out when the driver unloaded, and your rep had to sort out a substitute.

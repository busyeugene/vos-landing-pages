Why Teams Compare

Where an alternative to Cut+Dry starts to appeal

Cut+Dry calls itself customer-facing ecommerce and sales enablement for foodservice, and it serves distributors, manufacturers and restaurants from the same platform. That breadth is the product. It is also what sends some buyers looking at a Cut+Dry competitor instead.

📣

Someone else can buy that moment

Their own manufacturer pitch is targeted marketing that puts a SKU in front of an operator at the exact moment they are building an order. That is a real product sold to real brands. It is also your customer, mid-order, being sold to by somebody else.

💸

Cashback has to come from somewhere

Restaurants on the platform are offered cashback on eligible purchases from participating distributors and manufacturers. A lot of distributors will want to know exactly where that sits before they sign, and reasonably so.

🗓️

Launch is measured in weeks, not hours

Their own distributor page publishes a launch window measured in weeks. For a platform of that size it is a good result. It is still a quarter before the phone stops ringing.

Not Either Or

When VOS and Cut+Dry work together

VOS is not a Cut+Dry replacement and could not be one. It adds on, so a platform already carrying your payments and your storefront is no reason to rule this out.

🖥️

Let the storefront do the storefront

Discovery, search and a published catalog are real work, and none of it happens here. The accounts who want to browse keep browsing. The ones who just want Friday's order reordered get a quicker route to it.

💳

Payments and invoicing stay put

VOS never touches money. Nothing about adding it asks you to move how customers pay or how invoices go out, which is usually the part nobody wants to disturb.

📦

Still one place for the order to arrive

Whichever system you picked is where it shows up, over EDI, the API or QuickBooks. So nobody is watching two queues.

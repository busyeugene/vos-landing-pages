Each distributor that offers VOS loads the items it sells you, at the rates you settled with them. You pick the supplier, speak the list, and send it. No remembering who carries what, and no digging out last month's email to find a code.

A Number On What You Send

Each order you put in through the app carries a time and a number.

This is the part most supplier management software skips for a kitchen. The order you speak gets written out, you approve it, and it goes with its own number and timestamp. A supplier management platform that cannot tell you when you sent something is not much use on a Monday morning.

  • ✓You read the order right through before it sends
  • ✓Anything you send gets its own number, dated and timed
  • ✓A push notice or text confirms it reached the supplier
  • ✓Pulled off the floor halfway through? The app holds your place

Nothing To Administer

Supplier management portal software, without the admin.

A kitchen does not have a procurement department. There is no supplier management system here for you to log into, configure or keep in order. Your distributor does the loading on their side, and your side is an app you talk to. Anyone on the rota can be shown it in two minutes.

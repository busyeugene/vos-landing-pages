Sound Familiar?

Next week's order is somebody's best guess.

Most kitchens write the order from memory, on a Tuesday, in a hurry. It works out roughly. Roughly is expensive. Here's what it costs:

🎲

"We order the same amount every week"

Whether it's a quiet week or a holiday weekend, the order barely changes. So you're either short on your busiest days or throwing out product on your slowest.

🧠

"Only the chef knows how much we go through"

That knowledge lives in one head. When they're on holiday, whoever covers the order either over-buys to be safe or misses something entirely.

🗑️

"We're throwing out product every week"

Nobody planned to waste it. It's just what happens when you buy for a week you haven't looked at, using numbers you never wrote down.

Par Levels That Work

Demand and inventory planning software without a data team.

Set a par level for each item at each location: the amount you want on hand going into a normal week. VOS watches your live counts against those pars and flags what is short before you sit down to write the order. No models to configure and no analyst required.

  • ✓Par levels per item, per station, per location

  • ✓Adjust pars for a holiday week or a slow month

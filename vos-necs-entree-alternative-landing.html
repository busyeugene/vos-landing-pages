Why Teams Compare

What people mean by an alternative to NECS Entree

Almost nobody typing that phrase wants to tear out four decades of distribution software. They want one part of the day to improve. Three versions of that come up often enough to be worth separating, and only one of them is a NECS Entree competitor question at all.

⌨️

Ordering still means someone typing

Entree already offers customer ordering on web and mobile, and both routes have the buyer keying quantities against a search. Plenty of kitchens never take to that at the end of a shift, so the orders carry on coming by phone and your desk keys them anyway.

🔗

Everything new is inside the same system

When ordering lives inside the software that runs your company, changing it means changing that software, and it is their module to license and support. Some distributors would rather improve the order without opening that up.

🚚

Actually replacing it is a different project

If your real plan is to move off Entree entirely, be honest that the whole back office goes with it. VOS is not that, and we would sooner say so now than sit through the wrong demo with you.

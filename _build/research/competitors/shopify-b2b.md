## Positioning (verbatim)
- B2B page (https://www.shopify.com/plus/solutions/b2b-ecommerce, which now serves the content of
  https://www.shopify.com/solutions/b2b/enterprise; https://www.shopify.com/b2b shows the same page):
  **"Transform your wholesale commerce, delight your customers"** / "Design your B2B buying experiences
  on a customizable platform backed by **the best-converting checkout**." / "Enterprise wholesale
  features built right into the core of Shopify" / "Save time, reduce costs": one admin, "and even one
  store", for B2B and B2C. / "Go from zero to orders in weeks, not months".
- Named customers are brands (Brooklinen, Momofuku, ButcherBox, Allbirds, Dollar Shave Club, ...). No
  food-distribution or restaurant customer is named. Brooklinen quote: "Our B2B process was manual and
  time consuming." Their numbers ("300+ hours", "20X admin API rate limits", "90% faster", "40%") stay
  off the page.
- Help center (https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b): **"Shopify B2B is a suite of native features
  that lets you sell business-to-business (B2B)."** "use one store for both B2B and direct-to-consumer
  (D2C) sales, or create a separate B2B-only store." "you must set up your B2B customers as companies
  in your Shopify admin."

## What it does (concede all of it)
- B2B page features: "Company profiles" (buyers, locations, permission levels, payment terms, tax
  exemptions), "Customer-specific catalogs", "Volume pricing", "Quantity rules" (minimums, maximums,
  case packs, increments), **"Quick bulk ordering"**, **"Easy reordering"**, "Self-serve purchasing",
  payment terms and invoices, vaulted cards, PayPal, "Sales rep permissions", "Checkout to draft"
  (submit for review, approve before fulfilling), custom discounts, B2B theme, headless storefronts,
  Shopify Flow automations, PO numbers, B2B APIs, ERP integrations through partners (the page names
  two ERPs and an integration firm: claims.js bans those names; write "ERP connections through partners").
- Customer accounts (https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/customer-login-and-accounts): buyers sign in
  with "a one-time PIN that's sent to their email"; they can "View order history", **"Place reorders by
  duplicating a past order."**, pay, track orders, request returns.
- Store customization (https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/store-customization): "quantity rules,
  volume pricing and quick order list"; "Shopify's built-for-B2B theme, Trade"; gated store option.
- Store type (https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/getting-started/store-type): blended store = "a
  single Shopify store that's used for both B2B and D2C customers", "One Shopify admin", "Shared
  inventory"; dedicated store = "a separate Shopify store that's used only for B2B customers". Warns the
  choice is "not easy to change at a later time".
- Food: a Shopify Enterprise blog post (2025-08-29, https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/how-to-become-a-food-distributor)
  says "With Shopify, you can create a branded B2B storefront tailored to wholesale customers" and
  "buyers can log in to a custom B2B storefront with personalized pricing, minimum order quantities".
  No food-distribution product page exists. Do NOT write "not for food": food brands and distributors use it.

## ⚠ Phone and email orders: Shopify's own answer is the DRAFT ORDER, built by staff
- Draft orders (https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/checkout-and-orders/draft-orders): for **"orders
  that your customers send directly to you by phone or email instead of through your online store"**;
  "If a company submits an order with you outside of your Shopify store, such as by email or on the
  phone, then you can create a draft order for their purchase." Staff can send an invoice; "The order
  isn't complete until the customer submits payment after you send them an invoice or staff click
  Create order."
- Sales staff (https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/sales-staff): **"They can capture phone, email, and
  in-person orders into Shopify using draft orders."** Then send the customer an invoice to review and
  check out. "Sales staff permissions are available on the Grow, Advanced, and Shopify Plus plans."
- ⇒ A phoned restaurant order in Shopify B2B is typed into a draft order by someone on the team.

## ⚠ Plans (help center plan table, https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/getting-started/plan-features)
- "Shopify B2B is available on the Basic, Grow, Advanced, and Plus plans." Not on Starter or Retail.
- Active catalog assignments: **3 on Basic/Grow/Advanced, Unlimited on Plus.** **"Direct catalog
  assignment isn't available on the Basic, Grow, or Advanced plans."** (Direct company catalogs: Plus only.)
- Quick order list, Trade theme, Flow, draft order to invoice, checkout to draft, easy reorders, PO
  numbers, net terms, companies and locations: all four plans. Sales staff permissions: Grow and up.
- Pricing page (https://www.shopify.com/pricing): plan prices ARE published; Plus "starting at". B2B is a
  row on all four plans ("Up to 3 catalogs" vs "Unlimited catalogs"). Keep every figure off the page.

## ⚠ Mobile
- No dedicated B2B buyer app is described in the help center (the Shop app is mentioned only for
  consumer checkout). The B2B storefront is a website (mobile web). Do NOT assert "no app": third-party
  app builders exist. If a row needs it, the competitor cell is "Not listed on their site".

## ⚠⚠ VOICE / AI: third parties already sell it
- **Workd** (https://www.workd.com/integrations/shopify/): **"Workd takes phone orders and creates them in
  Shopify with the customer's account pricing."** "Some customers will always call." Built for
  distributors; lists Foodservice. Uses Shopify's Admin API. An AI phone agent: the caller sees no list.
- **LevelOps PDF to Order** (https://apps.shopify.com/levelops-email-to-order): "Turn PDFs, emails, &
  Excel files into draft orders to approve"; the team "reviews and approves every order". Also
  AutoApprove (https://apps.shopify.com/autoapprove): POs from PDFs, emails, spreadsheets, photos into
  draft orders. NEVER imply these read our files or that the two can be chained.
- **Shopify's own AI, Sidekick**, is a merchant (staff) assistant; secondary sources (Winter '26 / Spring
  '26 Editions recaps, e.g. https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/shopify-winter-2026-edition-sidekick-simgym-updates-guide)
  say it gained voice input in the admin/mobile app. Not verified on a Shopify page; not used on the page.
- ⇒ Never "the only voice option for Shopify". One-line concession in the quick-order FAQ.

## EDI / ERP / connection path
- B2B page: "Shopify integrates with your ERPs and existing tech stack"; APIs (Orders API, B2B APIs).
- **VoiceOrder Solutions + Shopify: no published connection found** (search 2026-11-09). Our order is
  NOT a Shopify order: it does not appear in the store's order history, reports, Flow or the chef's
  Shopify customer account unless your team creates it (e.g. as a draft order from our emailed file)
  or your setup takes it over EDI or the API. Whether it can is a demo question; never promise it.

## Angle for the VOS page
**An order, not a checkout.** Shopify B2B turns wholesale into a store: catalogs, a cart and the checkout
Shopify sells as "the best-converting checkout", often in the same store as retail shoppers. That suits
accounts that shop. For a chef who would rather call, Shopify's own answer is a draft order your staff
build by hand. VoiceOrder Solutions gives that chef an app to say the order and check each item; the
store stays for everyone who uses it. Second strand, Shopify-specific: a separate catalog per company is
a Plus feature (three catalogs on the other plans), while every VoiceOrder Solutions account gets its
own guide with its own items and prices.
Distinct from: inFlow ("No more phone orders" promise), Acumatica (free portal skipped), Odoo (Order
Again vs today's shortages), NetSuite (store add-on licensing), Zoho (portal takes payments), Business
Central (when the check happens), Choco (growth vs clean order).

# REKKI (rekki.com) — competitor research for `/rekki-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-10-10** (homepage, /for-customer-service, /app).

## ⚠⚠ THE PLAN'S PREMISE IS OUT OF DATE. READ THIS FIRST.
CLAUDE.md planned REKKI as "a supplier ordering app, the same SHAPE as VOS, not a platform",
based on the 2026-07 baseline in the Writing repo. **That is no longer what REKKI sells.**

Their homepage title is now **"REKKI - Office robots built for wholesale distributors"**, H1
**"Office robots built for wholesale distributors"**, subhead **"Automate orders, grow sales, and
serve customers with our suite of AI robots"**. The buyer is the distributor. The chef app still
exists at /app but is one nav item ("For Chefs") among For Customer Service, For Sales and Company.

So the angle is NOT "whose app is it". Eugene's decision 2026-10-10: **engage the accuracy claim.**

## The accuracy claim, verbatim, because the page turns on it
- **"Accuracy. Built with accuracy at it's core. 99.99% accuracy means just 1 error in 10,000
  orders."** (their typo, do not quote it)
- A stat block reads **"0.01% Error rate for your orders"**.
- **"Trained by you. Have full control — your team trains the robot to adapt to your unique
  business needs."**

⚠ **These are unaudited vendor figures. Never repeat 99.99% or 0.01% as a result or as fact.**
What is quotable is that they **publish an error rate at all**, and that is the whole argument:

> An error rate is the right measure for a system that infers an order from a message. VOS does
> not publish one for that step because nothing is inferred: the lines come off the account's own
> guide and the buyer approves them before sending.

⚠ **Do NOT imply VOS is more accurate, or 100% accurate, or that it cannot be wrong.** The honest
claim is a **different kind of check**, not a better percentage: theirs is machine reading verified
by the distributor's team, ours is a list confirmed by the person who wants the goods. And VOS has
its own hard limit, which the page must state: a phone call or voicemail to your desk gets no order
number, no timestamp and no stock movement.

⚠ **"Trained by you" is a real cost to name fairly, not a flaw.** Training the robot on your
business is why the accuracy is theirs to claim. VOS has nothing to train, which is a genuine
difference and checkable from their own copy.

## Pricing — ⚠ NOT parity, and it is in pounds
**"Starting at £10/customer. Includes OrderAI and InboxAI"**, with "ERP integration included" and
"On-call support for critical issues". So, like OrderEase, **REKKI publishes a floor and VOS does
not.** Concede it.

⚠ **The currency is a fact worth one careful sentence.** Priced in **GBP**, and the marketplace is
**"only available in the UK"** (their own FAQ). The chef app itself works "anywhere in the world".
So: do not write that REKKI does not serve the US, which is not established. Do write that the
published price is in pounds and the marketplace is UK-only, both read off their own site.
**Keep the £10 figure off the page**: a published price goes stale, same rule as OrderEase.

## ⚠ The chef app reaches distributors who never signed up
Their own FAQ: **"Do my suppliers need to be on REKKI? No - you can order from all your suppliers
on REKKI, whether or not they have signed up to the platform. Orders placed on the app are
automatically converted into email format and sent directly to your supplier's order email."**
And **"REKKI is completely free for restaurants."**

This is genuinely useful and must be stated accurately: a distributor may already be receiving
REKKI orders **as emails**, without any relationship with REKKI. That is a fact about how orders
arrive, not a criticism.

## Unaudited numbers — do not repeat as results
"90% drop in manual workload", "100% Complete automation with 1 integration", "0.01% error rate",
"99.99% accuracy", "24/7 On-call engineering". Describe the model, quote none of it as outcome.
Marketplace payments (card, Direct Debit, consolidated statements) are theirs and not ours.

## The angle for this page (must differ from all ten before it)
**Engage the accuracy number head on.** Competitors running comparison pages never do this; they
either ignore the rival's strongest claim or sneer at it. Taking it seriously is the differentiator.

> REKKI publishes an error rate. That is the honest measure for a robot reading an order out of a
> message. VOS has no error rate for that step, because there is no reading step.

Supports, all checkable:
1. **Something has to be read.** Orders arrive from every channel and the robot structures them;
   their team trains it and accuracy is maintained. VOS has the lines selected from the account's
   own guide, confirmed on the buyer's screen.
2. **Nothing to train.** Their copy says your team trains the robot. VOS has nothing to train.

**Concede generously:** OrderAI across every channel, InboxAI triaging email, MenuAI finding
upsell, the marketplace, a free chef app, published pricing, ERP integration included, 24/7
on-call support. VOS has none of it. Give REKKI the first rows.

⚠ **Do not reuse the Burnt angle** ("agents across the back office, who stays in the loop") or the
WizCommerce angle ("handled or ended"). Both are close. Keep this page on the measurement: an error
rate exists because there is something to be accurate about.

# TradeGecko (later QuickBooks Commerce): competitor research for `/alternatives/tradegecko-alternative`

**Researched 2026-11-09.** TradeGecko no longer exists, so this digest rests on (a) archived TradeGecko
pages from the Wayback Machine (snapshots dated 1 and 28 June 2020, fetched raw with the `id_` flag),
(b) Intuit's own sunset statements, quoted on a third-party page and in Intuit's community forum, and
(c) press coverage of the acquisition. Every quote below names its URL.

⚠ **tradegecko.com today 301-redirects to an unrelated punycode domain** (`xn--9l4b19k6tf.net`,
checked 2026-11-09). Never link to tradegecko.com from the page.

**Review 2026-11-09 (factcheck-bo H1): the retirement was STAGED, not one date.** Intuit's 2021 notice
(quoted at https://www.erpag.com/news/tradegecko-alternative): "June 10, 2022: We will retire this product,
and it will no longer be available." Unleashed (https://unleashedsoftware.com/blog/quickbooks-commerce-sunset,
10 May 2023): "In 2022, Intuit discontinued QuickBooks Commerce (formerly TradeGecko) for users outside the
US. Users inside the US had to either move to a QuickBooks Online + Commerce bundle or leave the platform
entirely." sku.io agrees (June 10, 2022: retired; US customers offered a version folded into QuickBooks
Online; customers outside the US lose it). So many users, everyone outside the US included, lost it in June
2022; 31 Aug 2023 is the last step only. No source dates the end of the B2B store itself: the page says
"when TradeGecko was retired, the store ... went with your stock records", with no date.
Page wording now (FAQ 3): "It stopped taking new customers in June 2021 and was retired in stages: many
users lost it in June 2022, and the last version closed after August 31, 2023."

**Safe page wording (original, superseded by the staged timeline above):** "TradeGecko, renamed QuickBooks Commerce after Intuit bought it, stopped taking new
customers in June 2021 and closed for good after August 2023." Never "abandoned", "left you stranded",
"shut down on you": Eugene, 2026-11-09, "be easy with it".

## What TradeGecko was (concede all of it: it was a full stock system)
Homepage, 1 Jun 2020 (https://web.archive.org/web/20200601221143/https://www.tradegecko.com/):
- "Automated and integrated operations for America's wholesale and multichannel businesses"
- "Organize your products, orders, payments, customers and insights, all in one secure system"
- "Complete inventory management: Keep track of your products as you sell, manufacture and restock across
  multiple warehouse locations and channels." / "Control over orders and fulfillments: Manage orders on
  multiple sales channels from purchasing through to shipping"
- Nav: Inventory Control, Order Management, Purchasing, Warehouse Management, Reporting, Manufacturing;
  Payments; Automation; integrations with ecommerce, accounting and shipping apps (claims.js bans most of
  those names: never list them).
- Industries listed include **Food & Beverage** (one of six); testimonial from a craft beverage maker.
- Homepage stats ("+50%" revenue, "24,000 users", "US$11.3 billion in orders") are unaudited: never repeat.
Pricing, 28 Jun 2020 (https://web.archive.org/web/20200628204013/https://www.tradegecko.com/pricing):
published monthly plans by sales-order volume and users; the B2B eCommerce Platform was included on
plans (made free on the two lowest plans "in response to COVID-19"); "Batch & expiry tracking" on the
mid plans and up. ⇒ **TradeGecko published prices. Keep every figure off the page**; no pricing row.

## ⚠ The part the page replaces: the B2B eCommerce Platform (customer ordering)
B2B platform page, 28 Jun 2020 (https://web.archive.org/web/20200628230132/https://www.tradegecko.com/b2b-ecommerce-platform):
- "Start selling wholesale online with your own B2B eCommerce Platform"
- "**Custom price lists**: Create unique price lists for each buyer, including discounts and minimum order

- "**Take payments**: Buyers can check shipping costs and pay online with secure credit card payments."
- "**Simple ordering**: Checking order status and repeating orders is much easier than over phone or email."
- "Each customer sees the products that you want them to, at the prices and discounts that you decide."

  on your store"; "request access for new buyers".

orders, with nothing retyped. It took orders at any hour.

## ⚠ TradeGecko's mobile app was for the DISTRIBUTOR'S team, not customers
Order entry app page, 28 Jun 2020 (https://web.archive.org/web/20200628230105/https://www.tradegecko.com/order-entry-app):
- "TradeGecko Mobile Sales app lets you manage your products, customers and orders from anywhere.
  **Available for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.**"
- "Sell anywhere: Create, edit, and manage sales orders, whether you are at a trade show or meeting with a
  customer. **You or your sales reps** will be able to conduct business from anywhere."

⇒ The customer's way in was the web store; the app put order entry in the REP's hands (iOS only).

## Overlap check (things VoiceOrder Solutions must not claim to win)
- After-hours ordering: the store took orders at any time. Tie, never a win.
- Per-customer catalogs and prices: tie (our order guides hold each account's items and prices).
- Customer reviews the order before sending: a cart is reviewed at checkout. Tie.
- Orders landing in the stock system with no retyping: TradeGecko wins (store orders were TradeGecko sales
  orders). Ours: over EDI or the API if the distributor's current system takes it (demo decides); by email
  the desk enters it from a checked file.
- Payments, branding, promotions codes, stock shown to buyers, order status, invoices: TradeGecko's store
  had them; VoiceOrder Solutions does not claim them (stock shown to customers, order status beyond
  confirmed and order history are unconfirmed in the KB). Concede in the table/FAQ.
- Voice: no evidence TradeGecko had any voice ordering (searched the archived B2B, home and app pages).

## Angle for the VoiceOrder Solutions page
An honest REPLACEMENT page, for one part only. TradeGecko's B2B store lived INSIDE the stock system, so
when TradeGecko closed, a distributor's stock records and its customers' ordering went with it in one
move. Most have since moved stock, purchasing and accounting to another system; VoiceOrder Solutions does
not replace that and never will. What it replaces is the customer ordering half: an app on the chef's
phone (iOS and Android) where the chef SAYS the order and checks each item, rather than searching a web
catalog, and it is a separate product that sits beside whatever stock system the distributor runs now.
Distinct from: inFlow ("No more phone orders" promise), Acumatica (skip the portal), Unleashed (reps),
NetSuite (another module), Fishbowl (first typing).

## Must NOT claim
- That VoiceOrder Solutions replaces TradeGecko's inventory, purchasing, warehouses, manufacturing,
  reporting, payments or accounting. Not an inventory or order-management system.
- That restaurants left TradeGecko or "switched from" it (template.js fails it); write "when TradeGecko
  closed" / "since QuickBooks Commerce ended".
- Any TradeGecko/Intuit knock; any price or TradeGecko statistic; any named integration or the name of the
  system a distributor moved to.
- That the store had no mobile access (it was a web store, likely usable on a phone browser: say "a web
  store", not "desktop only").
- That orders from our app land in the distributor's new system with no typing on every route.

## ⚠⚠ THE PHONE IS ON THEIR OWN LIST (the page's anchor)
Food distribution page, "Order Management":
> "Food distributors receive orders in a variety of formats, from those submitted through EDI or a web
> portal to those that come by phone call or email to your sales department. Capture, allocate and
> fulfill each one consistently while maintaining the flexibility to split cases, swap in
> substitutions and hold backorders as necessary."
⇒ Aptean's answer to a phoned order is to capture it consistently once it reaches the sales desk.
Somebody still takes the call. And split cases, substitutions and backorders are Aptean's job: the
page keeps them there (KB Q15: VOS holds the request only; the swap is decided in the distributor's system).

## ⚠⚠ AI: Aptean builds agents, including one that READS EMAILED ORDERS
- AppCentral (https://www.aptean.com/en-US/appcentral) and Aptean Intelligence
  (https://www.aptean.com/en-US/appcentral/aptean-intelligence): GenAI Query ("ask natural-language
  questions"), predictive insights, workspaces, AI agents; examples "Inventory and Expiration Management
  Agent", "Order Status and Fulfillment Agent". AI Agents page
  (https://www.aptean.com/en-US/appcentral/aptean-intelligence/ai-agents): "made from prebuilt
  industry templates or custom-built by our team through Aptean Intelligence as a Service (AIaaS)";
  "Some agents can run in the background ... Others should pause at key decision points, keeping a
  human in the loop."
- **Press release, 1 October 2026** (https://www.aptean.com/en-GB/resources/pressroom/press-release/lincoln-york-aptean-ai-agent-order-processing;
  the en-US URL redirects to the homepage): a UK coffee roaster "has gone live with a custom Aptean AI
  Agent that reads, interprets and processes incoming sales order emails". It "Reads and interprets
  orders ... even when requests are informal or ambiguous", "Creates the order automatically", and
  "**Flags what it cannot resolve**: After each run, it sends a summary of processed orders,
  assumptions made and any items needing human review". A second phase will "confirm processed orders
  back to customers".
⇒ Aptean's AI answer to messy orders is an agent on the DISTRIBUTOR's side that interprets after the
order arrives and hands its assumptions to staff. VOS moves the check to the kitchen, BEFORE sending.
Describe it as "a custom agent Aptean builds" (it is bespoke, one published case, a coffee roaster),
never as a standard product feature, and never say it does not work.

## Angle for the VOS page
**Aptean's food depth starts once an order is in the system; VoiceOrder Solutions changes who checks
the order, and when.** Aptean's own food-distribution page lists the phone call and the email to your
sales team among the order formats it captures, and its AI answer to a messy order is a custom agent
that reads emails after they arrive and hands its guesses to your staff. VoiceOrder Solutions puts the
check with the kitchen, before the order is sent: the buyer says it and confirms each line. Aptean keeps
traceability, catch weight, recalls, allergens, FEFO, pricing, substitutions, split cases and backorders.
Distinct from NECS Entree (typed vs spoken; inside vs beside) and NetSuite (where the order starts; web
store add-on; AI for staff).
- **Concede first:** every food-specific capability above; web portal / online store ordering; voice
  picking; route and cold chain; AI agents (including one that reads emailed orders).
- **What VOS adds (checked):** a spoken order in an app, confirmed line by line by the customer before it
  is sent; interruption recovery; 24/7; per-account guides from a spreadsheet; formats out.

# Pepperi (pepperi.com) — competitor research for `/pepperi-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-10-10** (homepage + /pricing). No prior entry in the Writing repo's
competitors file; Pepperi comes only from the October Airtable plan.

⚠ **"Pepperi" is not "Pepper".** There is already a `/pepper-alternative` page for a different
company. `names.js` is whole-word and its unit test covers exactly this ("does not match
'Pepper' inside 'Pepperi'"), so the two pages' exemptions do not bleed. Never let a sentence on
this page read as though it were about Pepper, and never cite one company's facts on the other's
page.

- H1: **"B2B Commerce Solution & Sales App for B2B Companies"**
- **"Brand & Distributors trust Pepperi for B2B eCommerce, Sales Rep Order Taking & Direct Store
  Delivery to: Increase Sales, Improve Margins, Accelerate Order-To-Cash"**
- Section head: **"Trusted by Leading Retail Brands & Distributors"**
- **"Unified B2B Commerce Platform"** — "Connect all your customer-facing channels & backend
  systems simultaneously", "All-in-one, web & native mobile app (iOS/Android)".

## The product is a suite, and the suite is the point
Their own nav lists: Platform, B2B eCommerce, Sales Rep Order Taking, **Route Accounting**,
**Retail Execution**, Mobile CRM, Inside Sales & Support, **Trade Promotions**, Pepperi Insights,
Pepperi Leap Forward. Plus "Operational Analytics" and an Integrations section.

The three that matter for our argument, because VOS has no answer to any of them:
| Module | Their words |
|---|---|
| Route Accounting & DSD | "Drive van sales & delight customers" — route planning, order taking and retail execution "on a single app that works even offline"; "van loading to end-of-day reconciliation"; returns, invoice printing, payment collection, POD; "route compliance through geo-tracking and time-stamped activities" |
| Retail Execution | "Drive excellence at the point-of-sale" — "eliminate stock-outs, validate compliance and manage assets", "Create store audits and capture data/photos to win at the shelf", "manage merchandisers" |
| Trade Promotions | "Increase order size & accelerate sales" — promotions across all channels, bundles, "personalized cross-sell/upsell promotions according to predefined logic" |

## ⚠ They DO have customer self-service ordering. Do not build on an absence.
B2B eCommerce: **"Let customers self-serve 24/7 through your branded B2B & wholesale eCommerce
portal or app"**, with **"buyer-specific experiences with their own catalog, pricing, discounts
& order history"**. That is account-level catalogs and prices, same as our order guides.
So the distinction is **not** that their buyers cannot order for themselves. It is that the
ordering is **typed or tapped in a storefront**, and that the storefront is one channel inside a
field-sales platform. None of it is voice.

## Who they say they are for
- **"1000+ medium and enterprise customers"**, "65+ countries", "$70 bn+ transactions per year",
  "24/7 follow-the-sun support". ⚠ **Unaudited marketing figures: never repeat them as results.**
  The one usable as positioning is their own phrase **"medium and enterprise"**, because it is a
  statement about who they sell to, not a performance claim.
- Logos are mostly consumer brands going to retail: SodaStream, Kimberly Clark, Hallmark, Hill's,
  Keune, Parsa, OGI Eyewear, FGX International, Image Skincare, Beauty Solutions, Nelt.
- Food names do appear: **Vintage Food Corporation** ("Third generation family-owned specialty &
  natural food distributor serving New England & NY/NJ") and **Eastland Food Corp**. So do not
  write that they have no food distributors. They have at least two and feature one.

## The angle for this page (must differ from all seven before it)
Pepperi's centre of gravity is **the visit**: a rep, a merchandiser or a van driver standing in a
store, auditing the shelf, running a promotion, loading stock, collecting payment. Everything in
the suite serves the selling organisation in the field.

VOS has nothing to do with a visit. Its whole premise is the reorder that happens when nobody is
there: a chef at the end of a shift who already knows what is low.

> Pepperi is built around the visit. VOS is built around the reorder that happens when nobody
> visits.

Two checkable supports:
1. **Their own stated market is "medium and enterprise."** VOS's own homepage says "Built for
   Small to Mid-Size Distributors. Enterprise pricing and enterprise complexity aren't your
   problem." Both are published self-descriptions, so this is a fair, sourced contrast rather
   than a jab.
2. **Breadth versus one step.** Route accounting, retail execution, CRM, promotions and analytics
   are a platform decision. VOS is the ordering step and nothing else.

**Concede generously, because it is all true:** route accounting and van sales, offline operation,
store audits and shelf compliance, merchandiser management, trade promotion logic, mobile CRM,
payment collection and POD. VOS has none of it and is not going to. Give Pepperi the first rows.

**Be fair about offline.** Their offline mobile app is a real engineering answer to vans in dead
zones. VOS does not claim offline ordering, only that an interrupted order saves and resumes.
Give them that row outright.

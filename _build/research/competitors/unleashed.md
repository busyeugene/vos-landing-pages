## Angle for the VOS page
- **Concede first and generously:** stock across unlimited warehouses, batch and expiry tracking, multiple
  units of measure, customer price tiers, the B2B Store (reorders, favourites, live stock, card payments,
  after-hours ordering), missing-order alerts in the CRM, warehouse app, accounting sync. Both columns tick.
- **What is specific to Unleashed:** it retired its rep ordering app in May 2024 and moved order processing
  into the B2B Store, explicitly so "sales teams on the road" can "process orders on a customer's behalf".
  In an Unleashed shop, then, an order that does not come from a customer typing into the store is entered
  by one of YOUR people: a rep at the store checkout, or the desk keying a call. VOS hands that entry back
  to the chef: they say the order in our app and check each line, and the rep's time goes to selling
  (KB: "Free Your Sales Reps to Sell"). H1: "Reps Sell, Chefs Order".
- **Distinct from:** Acumatica ("kitchens skipping your portal": we do not argue about who uses the store),
  Pepperi ("orders between visits": not about visits or repeat orders), Prophet 21 ("free up inside
  sales": the desk on the phone), NetSuite ("another module"), Syspro ("orders in writing"; food page =
  plant floor), Odoo ("what is short today").
- **Must NOT claim:** Unleashed has no customer ordering / no mobile / no after-hours ordering; Unleashed
  has no AI; nobody offers voice or voicemail capture for Unleashed; any VOS-Unleashed integration,
  marketplace listing or partnership; any Unleashed price figure or onboarding duration; that the rep app
  retirement was a failure (state it as their stated strategy); that VOS sees, counts, forecasts or
  reorders stock; that reps stop taking every order (scope: the orders a chef now places in our app;
  emailed orders may still be entered at the desk).

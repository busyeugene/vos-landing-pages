## ⚠⚠ THEY INGEST VOICE. Do not write that they have no voice.
The AI Order Entry Assistant, their words: **"Reads orders from emails, PDFs, spreadsheets,
scans, voice notes, and handwritten documents and converts them into usable order data."**

This is the trap that nearly sank the GrubMarket and NECS Entree pages, in a new costume. A page
claiming "they are typing, we are voice" would be factually wrong and easy to disprove.

The real, checkable distinction is **where the order becomes structured data**:
- WizCommerce **interprets an artifact a person already made** — an email, a scan, a spreadsheet,
  a handwritten sheet, a voice note left somewhere — and reconstructs the order from it. Their own
  safeguard: "Automatically detects errors and inconsistencies, giving your team a **final review
  step** before orders are created in the ERP." The last check is the **distributor's** team, on
  something the AI inferred.
- VOS has the order **authored as lines in the first place**, in the app, from that account's own
  guide, and the **buyer** approves the lines before it sends. Nothing is inferred, so there is
  nothing to catch.

> Read back from whatever arrived, or built as lines in the first place.

Also theirs: **"With 100+ pre-built ERP integrations"** the assistant "applies your ERP rules and
pushes accurate sales orders directly into your system". And it covers RFQs into quotes.

## ⚠ They ARE in food. Do not write "not built for food."
`/industry/b2b-food-and-beverage/` is a real page: **"Power High-Frequency Ordering Across Reps,
Buyers, and Warehouses"**, with substantive food-distribution workflows named as
**"Core Workflows Powering Modern Food & Beverage Distribution"**:
- Trade Discounts & Promotions (discount engine, promotion hierarchies, scheduled promotions,
  channel-level control, customer and product segmentation)
- High-Frequency Reorders (one-click reorders, rep-assisted reorders, multi-carts, self-serve
  checkout, live inventory sync)
- Multi-Warehouse Inventory ("showing availability only from the nearest or assigned warehouse",
  customer-to-warehouse mapping, **"Inventory on the Wheel"** for route-level stock)
- Custom Pricing Rules (flexible units of measure, account-level pricing, custom price lists,
  volume pricing)
- User Roles (login-only storefront, territory-based catalog visibility, role permissions)

And a named food customer: **Agha Ali, VP of Global Operations, Turkana Food**.

VOS's own verified "20-30 Min saved per order" stat. Theirs is a customer quote about reps; ours
is our homepage figure. Keep them far apart: never quote theirs, and do not let any sentence on

⚠ Note what their food page is built around: **reps placing orders faster, and buyers
self-serving in a storefront.** That is checkable from their own headings and needs no numbers.

## The angle for this page (must differ from all nine before it)
Not "voice versus typing" (they read voice notes). Not "not food native" (they have a food page
and food customers). Not "who stays in the loop" — that is the Burnt page's axis and must not be
reused.

The axis is **whether an interpretation step exists at all.**

WizCommerce's AI is pointed at the mess: whatever the account sent, in whatever form, becomes an
ERP-ready order, and the distributor's team gives it a final look. For a distributor sitting on a
pile of scans, spreadsheets and handwritten faxes, that is the right tool and this page should say
so plainly.

VOS does not read any of that. It changes what the account produces, so there is no artifact to
interpret and the person who knows the order is the one who signs it off.

**Concede generously:** handwriting, scans, spreadsheets and voice notes; RFQ-to-quote; a branded
storefront; a CRM; payments; AI product photography; their stated 100+ ERP integrations; and a
real food and beverage feature set with promotions, multi-warehouse and unit-of-measure pricing.
VOS has none of it. Give them the first rows of the table.

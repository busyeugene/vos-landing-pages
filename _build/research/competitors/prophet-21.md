## Angle for the VOS page
- **Prophet 21 is built around the order desk**: counter sales, inside sales, "the entire order entry
  process from one screen". Its own answers to customer ordering are a typed 24/7 web store (a separate
  product) and rep mobile entry; its AI (Prism) and its partner's document reader both work on orders
  that have already reached your people. However fast that screen, a phoned order still holds someone
  at the desk for the length of the call.
- **What VOS adds beside it:** the kitchen speaks its order into an app and checks each line, so the call
  never reaches the desk. P21 keeps Order Entry, counter sales, pricing, stock, WMS, books. The H1 is
  "Free Up Inside Sales" (P21's own term, and the KB's "Free Your Sales Reps to Sell").
- Distinct from NetSuite ("where the order starts") and NECS Entree ("typed vs spoken, you may not need
  an alternative"): this page argues about **who is on the line**, and about timing (a P21 replacement
  would land on top of a cloud move many P21 shops already face).

- No mobile order entry (Mobile Sales has it) or "P21 is desktop only".
- "Prophet 21 has no AI" or "Epicor will never add voice"; "nobody reads emailed orders for P21".
- "The only voice ordering for Prophet 21" (Workd; Prokeep for text).
- Any VOS-P21 integration, certification or partnership; that VOS uses the P21 API.
- "P21 is not built for food" (inferred from a list; not established).
- "The only ERP designed by distributors" or any Epicor/third-party number (70,000 requests, 250,000
  cloud users, 650 customers, 95% accuracy).
- Their price, an implementation length, or 2028 as a threat.
- That VOS forecasts, counts, picks or plans stock better.

# Acumatica (acumatica.com) — competitor research for `/alternatives/acumatica-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-11-09.** acumatica.com pages fetched directly (no 403). Several URLs that look
obvious are 404s: `/cloud-erp-software/distribution-edition/` and `/cloud-erp-software/mobile-erp/`.
No prior entry exists in the 2026-07 competitor baseline.

## Positioning (verbatim)
- Homepage (https://www.acumatica.com/): H1 **"An intuitive Cloud ERP solution to power your whole
  business"**; "Acumatica's AI-driven automation simplifies your workflows by handling routine
  processes". Homepage also shows "90% reduction in order processing times" and "20 hours saved
  weekly" as customer results: unaudited marketing figures, **never repeat them, even paraphrased.**
- Wholesale distribution (https://www.acumatica.com/industries/wholesale-distribution/): H1
  **"ERP Software for Wholesale Distribution"**; "Connect inventory, purchasing, warehouse operations,
  sales, fulfillment, customer management, and financials"; "Support B2B, B2C, marketplace, counter,
  and field-sales channels"; "Commit to accurate delivery dates with Available-to-Promise (ATP)".
  Its sector list (home goods, apparel, electronics, health and beauty, toys, lumber, hardware/HVAC,
  industrial, MRO, "Other Durable and Nondurable Goods") **does not name food**.
- Distribution management (https://www.acumatica.com/cloud-erp-software/distribution-management/):
  H1 "Distribution Management Software"; "Take control of inventory, purchasing, warehouse operations,
  and omnichannel sales." Cards: "Empower everyone with unlimited users".
- **No food or food-distribution page on acumatica.com** (nav lists Construction, Distribution,
  Manufacturing, Professional Services, Retail; the closest is Consumer Packaged Goods). Food and
  Beverage exists only as a filter tag on blogs and success stories. A partner (SWK Technologies)
  publishes a specialty food distributor story with FEFO picking and lot traceability. So: do not
  write "Acumatica is not built for food" (lots, FEFO via partners and the WMS are real), and do not
  write "Acumatica's food edition" either (none found).

## What it does for a distributor (concede all of it)
- Wholesale page tabs: Warehouse Operations ("Lot and serial tracking", "Mobile barcoding", "Shipping
  and EDI"), Demand Planning ("Use demand history, forecasts, current inventory, open sales orders,
  purchase orders, supplier lead times ... to make more informed replenishment decisions across
  locations"), Omnichannel Sales, Accounting ("AI-powered AP automation, expense management, and GL anomalies").
- Distribution management modules: Inventory, Sales Order Management, Purchase Orders, Requisitions,
  WMS, Distribution Requirements Planning, Amazon / Shopify / BigCommerce connectors, POS, CRM,
  Payments, Financials, Field Service, BI.
- Sales Order Management (https://www.acumatica.com/cloud-erp-software/distribution-management/sales-order-management/):
  "Optimize sales order entry and fulfillment"; "Acumatica checks customer credit limits before
  releasing sales orders"; "Manage complex pricing by warehouse, unit of measure, and other criteria";
  AI "intelligent cross-sell recommendations" ("also available for online orders"); blanket orders,
  ATP, order orchestration, counter sales.
⇒ Acumatica is a full system of record with demand planning and a WMS. VOS is neither; never imply
VOS sees, plans or counts stock better.

## ⚠⚠ Customer-facing ordering: THEY HAVE IT (a B2B portal). Do not claim otherwise.
- Customer Portal (https://www.acumatica.com/cloud-erp-software/customer-management/customer-portal/):
  **"Empower customers with secure, 24/7 self-service access to place B2B orders, manage accounts,
  and resolve support cases."**; "Enable frictionless online ordering with real-time inventory and
  personalized pricing"; "customers can view order details, repeat previous purchases, or cancel open
  orders"; "Empower sales, marketing, and support with unlimited users". No mention of mobile.
- Portal datasheet, version 20260512 (https://www.acumatica.com/media/2026/07/CRM_Customer-Portal-DS-GBE.pdf):
  "Customers browse a real-time product catalog with detailed item descriptions, multimedia,
  inventory levels, and account-specific pricing. Adding items to the cart is seamless, and at
  checkout, prefilled shipping and billing details save valuable time."; "Enable customers to browse
  catalogs, check inventory, and place orders anytime."
- Portal video page (https://www.acumatica.com/resources/customer-portal-video/): "Customers browse your
  catalog with real-time inventory, personalized pricing, item photos ... then check out".
- An older Acumatica datasheet (via search) says the B2B portal requires the Customer Management and
  Distribution Management suites. Not re-confirmed on a current page: do not state a licence requirement.
- Also: BigCommerce / Shopify connectors and "Business-to-business (B2B) and direct-to-consumer (DTC)
  storefronts" (wholesale page). Third-party B2B portals exist on the Marketplace.
⇒ The honest distinction: their customer ordering is **browse, cart, checkout** in a web portal (and
its 24/7 too, so **never** use "after hours" as a point against Acumatica). VOS is an order the kitchen
**speaks** into a phone app and approves line by line. And, specific to Acumatica: because users are
unlimited, giving every kitchen a portal login costs the distributor no seat fee. Seats were never why
a kitchen phones.
- **Customer ordering from a phone app: not listed on their site.** The portal pages never mention
  mobile; Acumatica's own app (App Store id952738765, datasheets 2019-2022) is for licensed EMPLOYEES:
  "mobile CRM, mobile sales orders", approvals. Write the cell as "Not listed on their site", never
  "no mobile ordering".

## ⚠ AI (native) — staff-facing, and it does not take customer orders
- AI page (https://www.acumatica.com/cloud-erp-software/artificial-intelligence/): AI Assistant "helps
  employees ask questions about business data in plain language"; **AP document recognition** "reads
  incoming vendor invoices, extracts key details, and creates bills automatically"; AI Automation "can
  update the detail lines of a record directly ... applying a discount or adding a free item across
  matching sales order lines"; anomaly detection; file tagging and case-summary agents; MCP ("Connect
  Claude, Gemini, Cursor, or other MCP-compatible AI tools"); 2026 R2 AI agents "take defined actions
  on eligible records"; AI Studio. **No voice feature and no AI sales-order entry from customer
  documents on that page.**
- AI Assistant FAQ (https://community.acumatica.com/acumatica-news-updates-2/acumatica-ai-assistant-faq-34846):
  "AI Assistant is limited to querying data through Generic Inquiries configured and exposed by your
  administrator."; "It currently cannot perform actions, update records, or access data outside of the
  configured inquiries."; "Only administrators have access to AI Assistant by default."; consumes "AI
  Units". A community member quotes a feature list with "Chat and voice interface for ERP interaction";
  **not on any official page found**. Do not write "Acumatica's AI has no voice".
- 2026 R2 press release, 1 October 2026 (https://www.acumatica.com/corporate-newsroom/press-releases/acumatica-2026-r2-release-with-embedded-ai/):
  "build agents with plain text prompts and generic inquiries"; distribution: "New Available-to-Promise
  functionality, intelligent picking strategies". No voice, no customer ordering.
- History: an Alexa skill announced at Acumatica Summit 2019 (GeekWire, 29 Jan 2019, via r.jina.ai)
  "lets customers do a variety of things via voice command", e.g. "checking inventory, looking up and
  approving expenses and updating opportunities". "Customers" there = Acumatica's licensees (staff).
  No order placing stated, no current page found. Do not cite; do not claim Acumatica "never did voice".

## ⚠⚠ VOICE ADD-ONS: third parties ALREADY sell voice ordering into Acumatica
- **Workd** (https://www.workd.com/integrations/acumatica/): "Voice AI for distributors"; "Enter
  orders, check inventory and look up customers by voice"; **"Your cloud ERP now answers the phone and
  takes the order"**; "Keep Acumatica as your system of record"; "Most teams are live in two to four
  weeks". Both REP voice entry AND an agent answering CUSTOMER calls.
- Lightspeed Voice (Acumatica Marketplace): call logging and summaries into Acumatica, not order entry.
- Artsyl OrderAction (Marketplace, certified for 2025 R1): reads customer orders from emails and scans.
⇒ Never write "the only voice option for Acumatica" or "nobody takes orders by voice for Acumatica".
The defensible difference: those are a rep speaking, or an AI answering the customer's phone call;
VOS puts the app in the **customer's** hands and the customer checks each line before sending. Concede
in an FAQ without naming anyone (named-integration ban).

## Implementation (no Acumatica figure)
- Delivered through partners (VARs); the AI Assistant FAQ: "contact your Acumatica VAR or CSM".
  APEX promises "faster implementation" with no duration. Acumatica's own case studies cite 3, 5 and
  6 months for particular customers; partners quote anything from 45 days to 12 months. **Use none of
  these figures**, and never set an ERP rollout against VOS's 24-48 hours as a knock: adding an
  ordering app and replacing an ERP are different jobs.

## EDI / connection path
- EDI is via partners: SPS Commerce "fully embedded" EDI on the Acumatica Marketplace
  (https://www.acumatica.com/acumatica-marketplace/sps-commerce-full-service-fully-embedded-edi-solution-for-acumatica/),
  also TrueCommerce and others. The wholesale page says "connected EDI". Concede EDI exists; do not
  say "native EDI".
- **VOS + Acumatica: no published connection.** VOS's verified paths are Email/PDF/Excel/Word, EDI,
  API, QuickBooks. Page says the two do not connect; which format an Acumatica site takes in directly
  is a demo question.

## Angle for the VOS page
- **Free logins, same phone calls.** Acumatica is the one ERP in the set whose licence is built on
  unlimited users, and it ships a real B2B portal (live stock, account prices, repeat orders, 24/7).
  So a distributor on Acumatica has usually already been able to give every kitchen a login at no seat
  cost. The kitchens that still phone are not short of a login; they will not browse, cart and check
  out. VoiceOrder Solutions adds a second way in for exactly those kitchens: they say the order into an
  app and approve each line. Acumatica keeps everything after that.
- **Concede first and generously:** financials and credit checks, lot and serial tracking, WMS with
  mobile barcoding, demand planning and DRP, the portal itself, unlimited users, EDI through partners,
  and a published starting price for distributors (a row they win).
- **AI:** Acumatica's AI answers staff questions and reads vendor bills; neither takes a kitchen's
  order. Scope it to those two features, not to "Acumatica AI" as a whole.

## Must NOT claim
- No customer ordering / no portal / no reorder / no 24/7 ordering in Acumatica.
- "No mobile ordering" (staff app creates sales orders; customer app is "Not listed on their site").
- "Acumatica's AI has no voice", or "the only voice option for Acumatica" (add-ons exist).
- Any Acumatica integration, Marketplace listing, certification or partnership for VOS.
- Their APEX figure, any implementation duration, or their homepage result figures.
- That Acumatica is not for food, or that it has a food edition.
- That VOS plans, counts or sees stock better; or anything pairing their price with a VOS price.
- A licence requirement for the portal (older source only).

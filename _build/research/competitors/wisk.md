## 3. Ordering / purchasing (THE OVERLAP)
Page: /features/order-management-system-bars-restaurants. H1 **"Automated Restaurant Ordering System"**.
**How the order is built**
- Par: **"Easily set and adjust par levels for each item or variation to ensure optimal stock
  levels and automate reordering."**
- **"All you have to do is click on prefill by par or by sales and WISK creates a purchase order
  in less than 2 minutes."**
- Order guide: **"Use an order guide to manage preferred items and streamline your ordering
  process with precision."**
- Suggested order: **"WISK automates the ordering process by analyzing your inventory levels, par
  levels, and historical sales data"** ... **"to recommend what and how much to order"**.
- Multiple suppliers per item: **"WISK enables you to assign multiple suppliers to an item,"**
  **"allowing you to compare prices and choose the best option for each order."**
- Help (app): **"WISK provides a recommended amount to order based on the Stock value"** and par;
  **"At this time, you can only pre-fill using the par levels you have set."**
- Help (web): orders built in the **Cart Builder** (Orders > Prepare Orders); **"You'll need to
  generate orders for each distributor separately."**
- ⚠ Pricing gates **"WISK suggested ordering"** to Multi-Outlet and Enterprise, not Single Venue.
  Par prefill appears in help docs without a tier note; do not state which tier gets what.

**How it reaches the supplier: EMAIL + SMS from WISK, or you place it yourself**
- Feature page: **"WISK makes it easy to order your food and beverage ingredients, whether you do
  it by SMS, email, or in your supplier's portal."** and **"send them to your suppliers via email
  or other preferred methods."**
- Help (web): **"To send the order to the distributor, click 'Email Order'."** It is
  **"emailed and texted (if a mobile number is provided) to the distributor contact."**
  **"The SMS message includes a link to a webpage where the order can be viewed"**.
- Open tracking: **"WISK tracks when an order email/text has been opened by the recipient."**;
  bounce alert: **"If the order email doesn't make it to the recipient, you'll receive an email
  notification."**
- Place it outside WISK: **"you can download a spreadsheet of the order"**, then **"place however
  you prefer (phone, in person, the supplier's web portal, etc)."** App option "Generate and Save":
  **"This generates the order in WISK, but it has not been placed."**
- Hide prices: checkbox **"Exclude price from purchase orders"**.
- Bar: a help article "Exporting your Purchase Order into the SAQ Web Portal (Quebec)" (title only).
**Does the supplier need to be on WISK?** No. Email/SMS goes to any distributor contact; the SMS
links to a view page. Nothing says the supplier must have an account.
**Supplier integrations are about INVOICES, not orders.** **"WISK Restaurant integrations focus
on importing invoices into your account(s) from your suppliers."** Named: Sysco (US, Canada),
GFS (US, Canada), US Foods (restaurant); Fintech, SAQ, BCLDB (bar); LCBO on /integrations.
Sysco US: **"invoices are sent to WISK and automatically uploaded into your account."** No order
transmission is described for any of them. EDI appears only for invoices (**"Sync with EDI"**) and
as **"EDI vendor integrations"** on the Enterprise tier.
**Receiving:** **"WISK matches received quantities against purchase orders, flagging discrepancies"**.
**Mobile:** ordering works in the app ("Generate and Send Emails / SMS"); the app pre-fills the
distributor's email and phone.

What the WISK order does NOT have that a VOS order does (supportable, from the above): it is an
email/text the rep reads and enters; it is not built from the DISTRIBUTOR's guide at the
distributor's current prices (prices are the venue's own from invoices); no order number from the
distributor's system. Phrase as "what the rep receives", never "WISK can't order".

## 4. Voice / AI / scan / camera sweep
- **voice, voicemail, speak, dictate:** ZERO hits on every page read, in the App Store listing and
  in a web search. No voice feature found.
- **AI:** pricing **"75 AI invoices/month"** (Single Venue), **"200-600 AI invoices/month"**
  (Multi-Outlet), **"human-in-loop invoice validation"** (Enterprise). Analytics page: **"Scan
  invoices and WISK's AI extracts line-item data and converts invoices into costs."** App Store:
  **"your new bar assistant"** (no AI feature described).
- **scan / camera / photo:** **"Users can use their phone's camera to scan barcodes and input the
  number of full and partial items"**; **"Smart Barcode Scanning"**; **"Snap, upload, and let WISK
  handle the rest."**; **"Scan, email, upload, snap a pic, or use EDI—whichever works best."**;
  **"Just take a picture, and let our team handle the invoice processing for you."**; **"From
  hand-written notes to item details, our tech and team catch it all."**
- Also: **"Bluetooth-Enabled Scale"**, **"Visual Method for Tracking"**, **"Offline Capabilities"**.

⚠ TRAP: no voice, but WISK IS an AI + camera product. "We use AI, they don't" is false (AI reads
their invoices). "They type, we speak" is TRUE for ordering but weak and reads as a knock: their
order is mostly pre-filled by par, so nobody types much. The honest contrast is where the order
LANDS and what it is built from, not keystrokes.
⚠ TRAP: their invoice copy says **"hands-free"** ("Line-item details sync straight into your
accounting software, hands-free"). VOS copy rules ban "hands-free" for VOS; do not echo it.

## 9. Real strengths a fair page concedes (biggest first)
1. **The count itself**: barcode scan, Bluetooth scale for partial bottles, offline, multi-user.
   Best-in-class for beverage. VOS counts nothing.
2. **POS-linked theoretical vs actual and variance**: catches over-pouring and theft.
3. **Invoice capture** (AI + team, EDI and Sysco/GFS/US Foods invoice import) and QuickBooks sync.
4. **Recipe and cocktail costing**, menu engineering, batch recipes.
5. **Ordering from par or sales to ANY supplier**, by email/SMS, with open tracking and receiving
   matched to the PO. Works with suppliers who have never heard of WISK; VOS works only with
   distributors on VOS.
6. Multi-venue central stockroom and transfers; published pricing and a money-back guarantee.

## 10. Sentences that would be FALSE ("WISK cannot / does not ...")
- ...send orders to suppliers (it emails and texts them).
- ...work on a phone / have a mobile app for ordering.
- ...suggest what to order (par and sales prefill; suggested ordering on higher tiers).
- ...use AI or the camera (AI invoices, barcode and invoice photos).
- ...have an order guide (it does), track whether the order was seen (open tracking), handle
  receiving, compare suppliers per item, publish pricing, or integrate with Sysco/GFS/US Foods
  (it does, for invoices).
- ...support multiple locations or commissary ordering (central stockroom).
- Safe true statements: no voice ordering found; supplier integrations described are for invoices,
  not order transmission; the order arrives as email/text for the rep to handle.

## Proposed angle
**WISK works out what you need from the count, and for the food order with a distributor that runs
VoiceOrder Solutions, you speak it into that distributor's own app, from your guide at their
prices, instead of sending a list your rep has to key in.** Distinctive to WISK: it is the
bar's count-and-cost system (scale, bottles, pours, liquor boards); VOS touches none of that and
only one of your suppliers' doors. Concede up front that beverage orders to liquor boards and to
suppliers not on VOS stay in WISK.

Candidate rows, WISK + VOS vs WISK on its own (both tick):
- Counts stock with barcode scan and a Bluetooth scale (WISK)
- Tracks pour cost and actual vs theoretical usage (WISK)
- Suggests order quantities from par levels (WISK)

Rows where only the VOS ordering job differs:
- Place the order by speaking it, at any hour, in your distributor's app
- Lines come from your distributor's order guide for your account, at the price they set
- The order lands in the distributor's system with an order number and timestamp (vs an email or
  text a rep reads; note phone and voicemail orders get no number either)
Caveat for every VOS row: only with distributors that run VOS.

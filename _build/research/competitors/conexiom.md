## ⚠ Phone, voice, text, fax: what Conexiom covers
- **Voice / calls: NOT among the formats listed** (Relay list above; Headless "PDF, Excel, CSV, image or
  text"). Site search found no voice, speech or call-capture feature (2026-11-09).
- **Its own answer to phone orders** (FAQ, https://conexiom.com/faq/): **"Can Conexiom help with phone
  orders? Yes. Many of your customers will create an order in their own system and then call the order in
  because they think it is faster than sending it by email. With Conexiom, orders sent via email will be
  in your system within minutes..."** ⇒ its route for a phoned order is to have the customer email it.
- **SMS and handwritten notes ARE read** (Relay FAQ). So a chef's TEXT can be read by Relay: never write
  "a text gives it nothing" or "it cannot read a chef's text". Fax: older Conexiom material (2015
  release, third-party profiles) says it converts faxed POs; the current site lists images and handwritten
  notes. Do not claim it cannot take fax.
- The Headless Platform lets a distributor's OWN apps and AI agents call Conexiom: so a distributor could
  build its own channel on it. Concede that in one line ("in early access").
- **Third-party voice/AI order tools for Conexiom:** none found (Conexiom is itself the AI order tool;
  search "Conexiom voice orders phone" 2026-11-09 returned nothing Conexiom-specific).

## ⚠ Never imply Conexiom can read OUR files, or that the two can be chained
VoiceOrder Solutions emails orders as PDF, Excel or Word. Conexiom reads emailed PDFs and spreadsheets.
It is tempting to say "point our email at Relay". **Never say or hint it.** No link, no joint support, no
test of it. The page says: email -> your desk enters it from a checked file; EDI/API -> may go straight in
if your setup takes it, demo decides. Nothing about Conexiom picking it up.

## Angle for the VOS page
**Orders that start in your codes.** Conexiom's job begins after a customer writes an order in their own
layout, words and part numbers: it reads it, then works out your part numbers, units and prices against
your master data, with rules taught per customer ("Reading a purchase order is solved. What the order
doesn't say is not: your part numbers, your prices..."). That is the right tool for accounts that email
POs from their own systems. A restaurant that phones has no such document; its own FAQ's answer to a phone
order is to have the customer email instead. With VoiceOrder Solutions the chef speaks from a guide you
built from your own item numbers and prices, and checks each item, so the order is in your codes before
it leaves the kitchen: there is no customer format to map for that account.
- Distinct from: Syspro ("its AI needs a written order; a caller gives it none": do NOT lead with
  "nothing written"; Relay reads texts anyway), WizCommerce ("whether an interpretation step exists":
  our app DOES match speech to guide items, so never say "nothing is interpreted"), REKKI (error rate),
  Business Central (when the check happens), Brightpearl (accuracy before automation).
- Concede: emailed/PDF/spreadsheet/image/handwritten/SMS orders; customer part numbers and units mapped;
  customer-specific price checks; plain-language exceptions; confirmations; AP invoices and vendor
  acknowledgements; Headless for your own apps and agents; EDI/XML/CSV output to 40+ ERPs (no number).

## Must NOT claim

## Angle for the VOS page
**"Let chefs keep talking."** B2B Wave is the closest job to ours: an account's own list of usual items,
one-click reorders, a Quick Order screen, standing orders, delivery cut-offs, all in a browser portal.
It suits the chef who likes to type and tap. Its own playbook for the chef who would rather talk is to
convert them to the portal or to keep a rep on the call who builds the order in the portal (assisted
ordering, "build the order, let them confirm"). VoiceOrder Solutions lets that chef keep talking, but
to an app: they say the order and check each item straight away. Keep both: typists in the portal,
talkers in the app. Concede most rows; we win on saying the order, checking it at once, and the
numbered order from a kitchen that used to call.
- Distinct from: inFlow ("no more phone orders" promise), Acumatica (kitchens skip the free portal /
  a login was never the problem), Unleashed (reps sell, chefs order), Odoo (Order Again vs today's
  shortages: never argue standing orders as "same order every week vs what is short").

**How an order is built** (help center):
- Three methods (ordering-options): **"Punchout Orders"**, **"Vendor Catalogs"**, **"Order Forms"**; orders intro also lists **"EDI Ordering"**.
- Order Form: **"Add items to a new order by clicking on the Plus icon and confirming the desired purchase unit."** Filter by **"items below or above par"**. **"We'll even recommend a Best Value purchase!"**
- Quick Orders: **"Craftable's Quick Orders functionality allows you to build out orders to your vendors through Craftable quickly."** **"The system defaults to selecting whichever vendor and pack size offer the Best Value."** (Multi-vendor price comparison, which VOS does not do.)
- Par: **"the minimum amount of each item that you want to have in stock."** Order to Par: **"New Orders will be built out, ready for placing with your respective Sales Reps."** Never automatic: **"This feature does not automatically order items that have fallen below par on your behalf."**
- Order Guides: **"Order Guides allow you to collate a list of items across all Vendor Catalogs within your Craftable account"**; require eProcurement: **"must have eProcurement as part of your subscription"**.
- Recurring orders: **"The platform automatically compiles your order, streamlining the process of placing it."**
- Punchout: **"Punchout ordering in Craftable allows you to order directly through your vendors online ordering portal"**; **"Build your order within the vendor's site, then click Transfer to Craftable"**; vendor must be one **"that we already integrate with"** (none named in the article).

**How it reaches the supplier:**
- Default is email + text to the rep: **"Select Place Order to submit the purchase order to your respective Sales Rep."** **"Your Sales Rep will receive a confirmation email & text message based on details provided."**
- managing-vendors: orders **"(that are not transmitted via either Punchout or EDI)"** are **"sent to your rep via email and/or text message."** **"if you enter a landline the vendor will not receive the order via text, though the system will attempt to send it."**

email means **"they will also be invited to join Craftable with a free vendor account"**. In the vendor portal the rep can

3. Spend control VOS does not have: approval limits by location/department/amount, budgets, contract pricing, off-contract and price-change flags, 3-way match before payment.
4. Multi-vendor comparison in one cart: Best Value vendor and pack size, cross-vendor order guides.
5. Par levels and order-to-par drafts tied to its own counts; recipe costing on live vendor prices.
6. AI on invoices (photo/PDF/email), a data assistant, morning ranked insights; voice for counts announced.
7. 60+ POS and 35+ accounting connections, SOC 2, SSO; hotel outlets, bars, banquets.

## ⚠ TRAP: an order placed in VoiceOrder Solutions is not a Craftable PO
Craftable's value is control at the PO: approvals, budgets, contract checks and 3-way match all hang off the order
being created in Craftable. An order a kitchen speaks into VOS goes to the distributor and does NOT exist in Craftable
(no connection). The page must say so plainly in an FAQ and must not imply the VOS order is matched, approved or
budgeted in Craftable. The honest scope: VOS for the routine reorder with a distributor that is on VOS, where the
operator is happy for that order to sit outside Craftable's approval flow; the invoice can still be captured in
Craftable the usual way. A group that enforces PO approvals on every order should be told this is a trade-off.

## Proposed angle
**Craftable runs a hospitality group's back office from the purchase order to the payment, built for finance and
purchasing to control spend across locations; VoiceOrder Solutions handles one moment Craftable leaves on a screen:
the kitchen's buyer placing the routine reorder with a distributor on VOS by speaking it, from that account's own
order guide at the distributor's prices, reviewed line by line before it is sent.**
Distinctive vs the batch: Craftable is the control layer (approvals, contract pricing, 3-way match) for multi-unit
restaurants, bars and hotels; VOS is the speed layer for the person standing in the walk-in. Concede the PO trade-off openly.

Candidate rows where BOTH tick (Craftable + VOS column and Craftable-alone column):
1. Orders reach the supplier in writing, not by phone (Craftable: email/text/EDI/punchout; VOS: order lands with the distributor).
2. Ordering from a saved list of the items you usually buy (Craftable order guides/order forms; VOS the account's order guide).
3. Order placed from a phone (Craftable mobile approvals/app per listing; VOS app). Check wording against "unverified ordering on mobile".

Candidate rows where only the VOS ordering job differs:
1. Place the routine reorder by speaking it, then review each line before sending (Craftable alone: built on screen; voice is announced for counts, not ordering).
2. Each order gets an order number and timestamp from the distributor's side, and the kitchen can order 24/7 without the rep (Craftable: also 24/7 by email; make the row about the distributor receiving it priced from the account's own guide, not about hours, or give Craftable the tick).
3. Saves 20 to 30 minutes compared with the same order by phone, and most kitchens place a first order the same day their distributor sets them up (Craftable: no published go-live; one group rolled out over 9-10 months; do not frame as Craftable being slow).
Rows NOT to use: "works with any supplier" (Craftable wins), "approvals/budgets" (Craftable only; belongs as concession), "AI" (both).

## 3. ⚠ TRAP: voice / AI / phone (every hit)
- **voice: ZERO hits** on homepage, /pos/, /pricing/, /inventory-management/, /profit-management/,
  /online-ordering/, /reservations/, /features/integrations/.
- **AI:** only the "Get an AI summary of TouchBistro" links (open ChatGPT etc.); no AI product feature found.
  A vendor blog (callsphere.ai) claims TouchBistro has "reservation-side AI assistants"; TouchBistro's own
  /reservations/ page shows none (two-way SMS and email only). Not used.
- **Guest phone ordering:** TouchBistro sells none on its own site. Third parties (e.g. an AI phone-answering
  vendor) sell phone assistants that enter guest orders into TouchBistro. Online Ordering is web-based:
  **"accept takeout/delivery orders directly through your website"**, also Google profile and social.
- ⚠ Consequence: on a POS page "voice ordering" reads as guests phoning in takeout. Say early and plainly that
  this is the kitchen's SUPPLY order to its distributor. Do not claim TouchBistro lacks guest phone AI as a
  point; just say ours is not about guests.

## 6. Real strengths a fair page must concede (biggest first)
1. **It runs service**: POS, tables and floor plan, tableside ordering, payments, offline mode, KDS, menu.
2. **Guest ordering**: web online ordering, delivery integrations, reservations, loyalty, gift cards.
3. **Supply ordering via its back office**: suggested orders per vendor, purchase orders emailed to vendors,
   online ordering to select vendors (Inventory Management); ordering from any vendor plus order guides
   (Profit Management); a mobile app that sends purchase orders.
4. **Costing**: counts by several people at once, recipe costing, food prep forecasting, invoice processing
   and price updates (Profit Management), accounting export.
5. **A broad partner list** for inventory, accounting and scheduling.

## 7. Sentences that would be FALSE ("TouchBistro cannot / does not ...")

- ...have a mobile app for ordering (iOS and Android, sends POs).
- ...suggest what to order (per-vendor suggestions; food prep forecasting).
- ...order online from vendors (select vendors; Profit Management says any vendor).
- ...keep order guides (Profit Management manages order guides).
- True and checkable today: no voice ordering of any kind on touchbistro.com. Date it in the sourcing FAQ.

## Angle for the VOS page
**"No new POS needed."** Someone searching "TouchBistro alternative" is usually weighing a new POS. This page
says plainly it is not a POS, and that if the pain is the food order to a distributor, that one step changes
without touching the POS that runs service. TouchBistro's supply ordering lives in back-office add-ons
(quoted separately) or partner tools; whichever you run, or none, the order to a distributor that offers our
app can be said aloud, checked line by line from that distributor's guide at your account's prices, and sent
with an order number. Different from the taken angles: not the count (WISK), not approvals (Craftable), not
planning (Apicbase/R365), not the supplier call alone (MarketMan), not invoices (MarginEdge/Orderly), not
"whose guide" as the main point (xtraCHEF). Its own facts: POS vs back office, guest orders vs supply orders,
and "select vendors online, the rest by email".

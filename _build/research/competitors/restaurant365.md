## 3. Purchasing / ordering (the overlap — read this section before writing)
**Marketing pages:**
- **"Create purchase orders based on up-to-date inventory data for smarter buying"**; **"Rely on forecasts and PAR levels to minimize waste and prevent stockouts"**; **"Speed up purchasing with seamless EDI integration and automated workflows"** (/inventory/purchasing-receiving/).
- FAQ: R365 **"connects with major food and beverage distributors, allowing you to place orders, receive deliveries, and update inventory automatically."** (/inventory/purchasing-receiving/).
- **"Generate AI-driven purchase orders based on actual demand to reduce waste and over-ordering."** (/inventory/, /ai/).
- **"Use pre-built order guides or create your own"**; **"Order smarter with shopping lists and POs"** (/inventory/inventory-management/).
- Pricing page, Inventory & Purchasing: **"Purchasing with vendor integrations to control spend"**, **"Price alerts"** (/pricing/).

**How an order is built (docs):**
- Web PO form: **Add All Primary Items** / **Add All Vendor Items** / Add Item, search bar. **"Suggest Qty"** **"will run a built-in algorithm using usage per $1000 and forecasted sales"**, after choosing consumption and buffer days. Par Qty = **"The minimum amount needed in inventory"**, set on Shopping Lists (defaults to 0). On Hand starts as a theoretical value, editable. Item price can come from **"Order guide Integration"**, a contract price or the vendor item price (docs/purchase-order-form).
- Mobile (R365 App): **"Users can create, edit, review, and submit purchase orders from within the R365 App."** Single-vendor or multi-vendor (from a shopping list). Start from a template or a vendor; **"Only the templates assigned to the selected location will be available"**; templates are built on the web. Add items by all vendor items, all primary items, **"Scan barcode - When barcode scanning is enabled, add items by scanning the barcode"**, or manually. Fill by Suggested Quantity or **Autofill to Par** / **Fill to Par**. Then Review, Save or Send (docs/r365-app-creating-an-order, /r365-app-orders-overview).
- Permissions gate it: Purchase Orders View / Send/Submit / Edit / Create / Delete (docs/r365-app-orders-overview).

**How it reaches the vendor:**
- **EDI vendors:** **"The Purchase Order will be submitted directly to the vendor's system to be processed."** Set up by ticket to R365 Support; every EDI item needs a case-based Purchase UofM. **"Only select vendors support Purchase Order EDI Integrations."** (docs/purchase-orders-submit-orders-via-edi).
- **Everyone else, by email:** PO goes to **"the email address on the vendor record"** and a vendor contact keys it in. No email on file: **"The purchase order will not send."** Mobile: **"Tapping Send will send the order as an email to the vendor email on file."**
- **US Foods (API):** Send redirects to the US Foods website to review and fix the order (7-digit numeric item numbers required) (docs/r365-app-creating-an-order).
- After sending, changes go to the vendor directly: Undo Send only reverses it inside R365.
- **Vendor integration list** (~205 vendors; columns "Vendor", "Multi-Invoice", "Purchase Order", "Order Guides"): PO EDI + Order Guides listed for **Sysco, Sysco Canada, US Foods, Performance Food Group, Gordon Food Service (US + Canada), Shamrock Foods, Ben E. Keith, Reinhart Foodservice**, plus McLane Foodservice, Sygma, Charlies Produce, DMA, MBM and others. Freshpoint, Ed Don, Labatt, Imperial Dade: invoice + PO. Cheney Brothers, Chef's Warehouse: invoice EDI only. Most of the ~205: invoice EDI only (docs/vendor-integrations-list).
- **The vendor does NOT have to be on R365.** Any vendor with an email address can receive a PO. This is a real advantage over VOS, which only works with distributors who are on VOS. Concede it.

## 4. ⚠ TRAP — keyword sweep (voice, voicemail, speak, AI, dictate, scan, camera, photo)
- **voice / voicemail / speak / dictate / speech:** **NO hits** on any page or doc read, and two web searches found nothing. No voice feature as of 2026-10-27. The "they type, we speak" contrast is factually available here, but say it carefully: R365 orders are built by template, par, suggested quantity, barcode scan or manual entry, not by voice.
- **AI (many hits, none about voice):** R365 AI (**"the intelligence engine built for the restaurant back office"**); **AI Dashboards** (**"Ask a question, get a dashboard. R365 AI generates custom reports, visualizations, and what-if scenarios from a simple prompt."**, /inventory/); **AI Advisor** (Early Access, ask questions across finance and operations); **AI Scheduling** (Early Access); **"AI-driven purchase orders based on actual demand"**; **"AP Capture AI"** OCR on invoices; AI timecard review; **"Secure Data Share"** (Early Access); Chef's Table early-access programme (/ai/, /, /accounting/ap-automation/).
- ⚠ **TRAP: "AI-driven purchase orders"** means R365 already suggests WHAT to order. Never write that VOS is smarter about quantities, or that R365 makes you work out the order by hand. VOS does no forecasting. VOS's difference is HOW the order is placed with the distributor, not what goes in it.
- ⚠ **TRAP: natural-language prompts exist** (AI Dashboards, AI Advisor). Typed, for reports, not ordering. Do not write "R365 has no conversational AI".
- **scan:** barcode scanning to add items to a mobile order (when enabled); paper invoices **"scan and upload"** (/accounting/ap-automation/ FAQ).
- **camera / photo:** R365 Red App "Upload Files" can **take a photo** of an invoice or credit memo into Documents to Process (docs, via search); **"easy mobile uploads"** of invoices (/accounting/ap-automation/). Limitation they publish: **"invoices the system cannot fully read still require human review"**.
- **chat:** "Logbook & Chat" is team messaging, not ordering.

## 9. Real strengths a fair page must concede (biggest first)
1. **It is the restaurant's accounting core**: GL, AP automation, banking, financial reporting, budgeting. VOS does none of it.
2. **Inventory, recipes, prep and food cost**: counts, actual vs theoretical, recipe costing. VOS counts nothing.
3. **It already builds the order**: par, suggested quantities from forecast sales, AI-driven POs, templates, shopping lists, multi-vendor orders.
4. **It sends to almost any vendor**: EDI to the big broadliners (Sysco, US Foods, PFG, GFS, Shamrock, Ben E. Keith, Reinhart), email to everyone else. The vendor does not need to join anything.
5. **Receiving, invoice matching and credits** flow straight into inventory and AP.
6. **Labor, scheduling, payroll, HR** in the same system; mobile app for approvals and counts.

## 10. Sentences that would be FALSE ("Restaurant365 cannot / does not ...")
- ...have a mobile app for ordering (it does: create, review, send POs from the R365 App).
- ...send orders to distributors electronically (EDI to select vendors; API to US Foods).
- ...work with your distributor unless they join (email works with any vendor).
- ...suggest quantities / use pars / use order guides (Suggest Qty, Fill to Par, order guide integration).
- ...use AI (R365 AI, AI Dashboards, AI POs, AI Advisor).
- ...scan (barcode to add order items; invoice scan/photo).
- ...handle multiple vendors in one go (multi-vendor orders from a shopping list).
- Safe, as of 2026-10-27: R365 publishes no voice ordering. Its email path is a PO that **someone at the vendor keys in** (their own doc). It needs a vendor email on file or the PO **"will not send"**.

## Proposed angle
**"Keep Restaurant365 as the back office that knows what you should order and what it cost; add VoiceOrder
Solutions for the minute where you actually place that order with a distributor who is on VoiceOrder Solutions,
by speaking it into the app and checking each line before it goes."**
R365 is the system of record and the planner; VOS is only the ordering step with one distributor. No connection
between them: the R365 PO and the VOS order are separate (say plainly that the kitchen re-enters nothing into
R365 automatically, and that invoices still reach R365 the way they do today).

**Rows where both tick (`R365 + VOS` vs `R365 on its own`):**
1. Accounting, AP and financial reporting stay where they are (tick / tick).
2. Inventory counts, recipes and food cost (tick / tick).
3. Suggested quantities from pars and forecast sales (tick / tick: R365 does this; VOS adds nothing here).

**Rows where only VOS's ordering job differs:**
1. Speak the order to your distributor and review each line before it sends (tick / "Typed, scanned or filled from a template").
2. Order lines come from your distributor's own order guide, at the price your distributor set for your account (tick / "Order guide integration with listed vendors" — do not give R365 a cross; say it plainly).
3. Each order to that distributor gets its own number and timestamp, any hour, and most kitchens send a first one the same day their distributor sets them up (tick / R365 POs also carry PO numbers: make the row about same-day start or 24/7 voice, not about numbering).

⚠ Watch row 3: R365 POs have PO numbers and statuses, so "a number on every order" alone is not a VOS win.
⚠ Every VOS cell is scoped to "a distributor on VoiceOrder Solutions". R365 reaches any vendor with an email;
VOS reaches only participating distributors. Say so in an FAQ.

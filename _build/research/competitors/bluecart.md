# BlueCart (bluecart.com) — competitor research for `/bluecart-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-10-10.** No prior entry existed in the Writing repo's competitors file.

## Positioning (verbatim)
- H1: **"Sell to 48,000+ Restaurants with Our Software for Distributors"**
- Subhead: **"Join the largest wholesale marketplace in the food and beverage industry."**
- It describes itself as a **marketplace** and a **vendor platform**, not only as software.
- FAQ: the platform avoids **"enterprise-level pricing or complexity"** and is
  **"built to fit your existing workflow."**

## What it sells (verbatim)
| Feature | Their words |
|---|---|
| Order Management Solution | Receives and manages buyer orders from one dashboard; confirm, fulfill and track digitally. Positioned as replacing phone and email ordering. |
| Endless Aisle Marketplace | A vendor platform where suppliers upload catalogs and get featured to buyers. "The largest and fastest-growing vendor platform in the industry." |
| **Pricing Insights** | **Labels products "Great Value", "Good Value" or "Fair Value" to help vendors price competitively.** |
| Customizable Delivery Zones | Vendors pick states and cities, so products show only to buyers in those zones. |
| Incentivize Repeat Business | Free delivery to first-time buyers, then discounts and promotions. |
| Others | Vendor Management, BlueCart Pay & Wholesale Invoicing, B2B Online Ordering, Wholesale CRM & Sales Rep App, Digital Catalog, Analytics & Reporting. |
| Restaurant side | Restaurant Inventory Management, Procurement, Food Costing, F&B Inventory. |

## Scale claims (vendor self-reported, NOT audited — never state as fact)
48,000+ restaurants, 100K+ businesses, $2B+ transaction volume, $32M+ raised.

## The angle for this page (must differ from every page so far)
BlueCart's own framing is a **marketplace you join**. The honest contrast is **whose storefront it
is and who else is on the shelf**:
- "Endless Aisle" means the buyer is browsing a catalog that reaches beyond you, and BlueCart's
  own pitch to distributors is access to 48,000+ restaurants, which is a demand-side argument.
- **Pricing Insights labels your products Great / Good / Fair Value.** The platform grades your
  pricing, and that grading is a feature sold to vendors. On a marketplace that is a service; for
  a distributor who competes on service and relationship rather than on price, it is the thing
  they would least like sitting next to their catalog.
- VOS has no marketplace, no discovery and no price grading. The account sees the guide you built
  at the prices you set, and there is no one else in it.

**Concede plainly, because it is the whole point of their product:** if what you want is new
customers, a marketplace can introduce you to them and VOS cannot. VOS only serves accounts you
already have. Say that in the table and in an FAQ.

## Angle for the VOS page
- **Concede first:** Brightpearl runs stock across locations, the warehouse, price lists per account,
  invoicing, accounting, demand-based purchasing, EDI orders from trading partners, and online ordering
  through partner webstores and portals. Its Automation Engine is real.
- **The angle:** Brightpearl is built around CHANNELS: orders from connected webstores, marketplaces and EDI
  partners flow in and its Automation Engine takes them from there. Its wholesale side is written for brands
  selling to stores. A food distributor's restaurant kitchens mostly phone, and a phoned order is the one
  channel with no connector: someone takes the call and types it in (Brightpearl's own wholesale page says
  you "take orders via phone"). VoiceOrder Solutions gives kitchens their own ordering channel: the chef
  says the order in our app, checks every line, and it arrives written, numbered and timed. Over EDI or the
  API it can go straight into Brightpearl if your setup takes it (demo decides); by email, your team enters
  a checked list rather than a call.
- **Who it fits (say honestly):** Brightpearl's pages speak to growing retailers and wholesale brands. A
  food distributor running it for wholesale keeps it, and adds us for the accounts that call.
- **Must NOT claim:** Brightpearl cannot take phone orders; no customer ordering; no portal; "not for food";
  "Brightpearl has no AI"; any Brightpearl integration, app store listing or partnership; their 120 days or
  any of their percentages and hour counts; their customer counts; that VOS sees, counts or plans stock
  better; naming the ecommerce platforms, marketplaces, EDI providers or accounting tools it connects to.

## Re-angle 2026-11-09
- **The angle is now accuracy before automation**, not channels. Brightpearl's Automation Engine acts on
  whatever order it is given; a product misheard on a call and typed in looks ordinary to its rules and
  reserves stock, goes to picking and reaches the invoice like a correct one. With VoiceOrder Solutions the
  chef says the order and checks each item on their own screen before sending, so the person who knows the
  kitchen gets a chance to catch a slip before the Automation Engine acts.
- **The channels argument belongs to the Cin7 page** (`/cin7-alternative`); do not bring it back here.
- **Guard (verification round 2026-11-09):** never let a line imply an app order cannot be wrong. The page
  keeps the FAQ "Can the app still send a wrong order? Yes", and the comparison H2, lead, testimonials and
  trust line were reworded from "the order the chef meant" / "right before it goes in" / "fixes items" to
  "an order the chef went over" / "every item is on their own screen to check" / "checks each item". The
  exceptions FAQ concedes that a rule can catch some mistakes (an amount far above normal).

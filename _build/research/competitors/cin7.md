# Cin7 (cin7.com) — competitor research for `/alternatives/cin7-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-11-09.** cin7.com pages were read directly; the Cin7 Core help center
(help.core.cin7.com) returns HTTP 403 to direct fetches, so those articles were read through the
r.jina.ai reader (same page, rendered to text). No prior entry exists. Quotes below are verbatim as
returned by the fetch; where only a summary came back, the line says so.

## Two products: scope every claim to the one it is true of
- Homepage (https://www.cin7.com/): **"Inventory Management Software for Modern Sellers"** /
  "Say goodbye to inventory chaos. With Cin7 IMS, you can connect suppliers, warehouses, and sales channels".
  Product menu: **Cin7 Core** "Robust out of the box features to streamline operations" and **Cin7 Omni**
  "More customizable, best for niche use cases".
- Cin7 blog, "Cin7 Core or Omni: Which One Is Right for Your Business?", **27 May 2026**
  (https://www.cin7.com/blog/cin7-core-or-omni-which-one-is-right-for-your-business/): Core is a
  "turnkey, out-of-the-box solution ideal for SMBs"; Omni is a "highly customizable platform designed for
  enterprise-level scaling". Core lists "A portal for B2B customers" and "Point of sale technology"; Omni
  has "built-in support for complex workflows, including 3PL and EDI". No implementation timeframes.
- Core (https://www.cin7.com/solutions/core/): "Inventory Software Built for Growing Product Businesses";
  "Manufacturers, retailers, e-commerce sellers, and wholesalers use Cin7 Core"; FAQ "a top inventory
  management software for small to medium sized businesses".
- Omni (https://www.cin7.com/solutions/omni/): "Your Channels, Your Locations — All Connected"; "Omni is
  ideal for businesses with complex operations, such as multi-entity companies"; "Integrate native EDI and
  3PL providers to streamline your supply chain".
- Core was formerly DEAR Inventory (third-party listings, e.g. Capterra "dear-inventory" URLs redirect to
  Cin7 Core). Not used on the page.
⇒ **A small or mid-size food distributor most likely runs Cin7 Core** (SMB, published tiers, wholesale on
every plan). Omni is the multi-entity / enterprise one. The page speaks to "Cin7" generally and scopes
the product-specific facts (IDR = Core only; native EDI = Omni; partner EDI = Core). FAQ 3 handles Omni users.

## What it does for a distributor (concede all of it: both columns tick)
Stock across unlimited locations; batches, serials, expiry, FEFO; recalls and audits; purchasing and
supplier management ("automate purchase orders, track supplier performance"); WMS with picking (Advanced
Warehouse Management add-on on Pro); MRP and production; reporting; accounting connections; price tiers,
order minimums, quantity multiples (B2B FAQ); ForesightAI demand forecasting (add-on).
⇒ Cin7 IS the stock system. VOS never sees, counts, plans or reorders stock better. Canonical stock
wording only.

## ⚠⚠ AI that turns a customer's PO into a sales order (IDR) — concede it
- AI page (https://www.cin7.com/features/ai/): ForesightAI (forecasting), **AMA "Ask Me Anything" AI
  assistant** (answers questions about sales and inventory, for staff), **Intelligent Document Recognition
  (IDR)** "reads POs and turns them into sales orders in real-time" (FAQ, per the fetch), AI product
  descriptions, Automations. No voice. No customer-facing AI.
- IDR page (https://www.cin7.com/features/sales/idr/): "IDR currently supports PDFs, PNGs, BMPs, and
  JPEGs"; "Upload entire batches of documents at once"; "Review automatically populated fields, make quick
  adjustments if needed"; **"Currently, Cin7 IDR is exclusively available for all Core customers"**; an
  "affordable add-on to any Cin7 Core subscription" (price published: off the page). "4-8 hours every week"
  saved: their unaudited figure, do not repeat.
- Help center, "Extract sale data with Intelligent Document Recognition (IDR)" (read via r.jina.ai,
  https://help.core.cin7.com/hc/en-us/articles/10553586503439): staff go to Sales > Import > IDR (or Google
  Drive); documents show as Draft then Recognized; staff check the mapping, "Ready for approval" makes a
  "Sale awaiting approval", then "Review and approve" (add the sales rep, match products). "Only one invoice
  should be included per document." PDF/JPG/BMP/PNG, 5 MB max.
- Newsroom, 21 Aug 2024 (https://www.cin7.com/newsroom/cin7-launches-new-ai-capabilities/): ForesightAI and
  IDR, which "captures, interprets and extracts key data from a purchase order document (pdf)".
- 2026 news found by search only: Rillet accounting partnership (Mar 2026), new CEO (Apr 2026), Inventoro
  forecasting acquisition. No AI agent for order intake, nothing voice.
⇒ IDR helps with kitchens that SEND a PO file. A kitchen that calls sends no file. ⚠ Syspro's page already
argues "AI needs a written order", so this is a CONCESSION here (a coexistence card), not the angle.

## Angle for the VOS page
**Cin7 is built to connect channels** (its own homepage line: "connect suppliers, warehouses, and sales
channels"): online stores, marketplaces, retail chains over EDI, a POS counter and an add-on B2B web
portal written for a brand restocking retailers' shelves. Every one of those hands Cin7 an order as data.
The channel a food distributor leans on most, the kitchen that calls each morning, is not one Cin7
connects: the call is typed into a Cin7 sale by someone at the desk. **VoiceOrder Solutions adds that
kitchen channel**: the chef says the order into a phone app, checks each line and sends it, and Cin7 keeps
the stock, batches and every other channel. H1 "Not A Cin7 Alternative: A Kitchen Ordering Channel".
Second strand (one card only): Cin7 is written for product sellers (retail, e-commerce, brands, makers);
its food page talks batches, FEFO and recalls, and the fetch found no restaurant or distributor mention.
Distinct from the 8 ERP pages: NetSuite = no extra module; Syspro = orders in writing (AI needs a written
order); Business Central = check before sending; Prophet 21 = inside sales; Odoo = today's shortages;
Acumatica = kitchens skipping your portal; Sage X3 = the kitchen's side of a partner build; Aptean = food
depth. Here the argument is CHANNELS.

## Must NOT claim
- "Cin7 has no mobile ordering" (the portal opens in a phone browser) or "no AI" (IDR, AMA, ForesightAI).
- "The only voice option for Cin7" (Alexa/Zapier, phone agents, OCR tools exist).
- That VOS connects to, integrates with, or is a partner of Cin7; that VOS orders count against a Cin7
  plan's integrations or sale order volume; any Cin7 price or onboarding figure; "700+".
- That VOS sees, counts, forecasts or reorders stock; anything about substitutions.
- That Cin7 is "too big" or "only for retail": it lists wholesale and food, and serves SMBs.
- That a kitchen cannot use the portal: some accounts will, and should keep it.
- Retailer or marketplace brand names (claims.js bans product names; not needed).

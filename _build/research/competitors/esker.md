## Angle for the VOS page
**Where restaurant orders start.** Esker's own order-to-cash pitch is to protect cash "upstream", starting
when an order reaches the shared inbox (email, fax, EDI, portal, punchout, mobile) and running through
credit, invoices, claims and collections. Its own capture page states the premise: let customers order
"any way they want" and read whatever they send. A restaurant's order starts further upstream still: at
the chef's shelf, and it often travels as a call or a text to a rep's phone, which never lands in the
shared inbox. VoiceOrder Solutions adds that first step: the chef says the order in our app and checks each
item; Esker keeps everything from the inbox onward.
- Not Syspro's "its AI needs a written order" (do not lead with the caller giving AI nothing), not Business
  Central's "when the check happens", not Brightpearl's "accuracy before automation", not Conexiom's
  "mapping the customer's codes". The Esker-specific facts: CFO / order-to-cash suite, "upstream", the
  shared inbox, "any way they want", and Paulig's "order volumes too low to switch to EDI".

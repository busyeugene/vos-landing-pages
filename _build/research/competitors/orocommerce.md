## ⚠⚠ AI and VOICE: Oro already has a buyer-facing assistant that takes SPOKEN requests
- AI page (https://oroinc.com/b2b-ecommerce/ai-ml-open/, published 2026-11-06): **"Many buyers still prefer
  sending orders or calling their rep instead of navigating a portal. AI makes that familiar habit part of the
  digital process. Through a simple chat, customers can check the availability of past orders, see substitutes
  when items are out of stock, and even place a new order on the spot."**
- **AI SmartAgent**: "gives customers a self-serve assistant that can check stock, confirm pricing, or place a
  reorder in plain language"; product page: "creates quotes, shopping lists, or orders using natural language."
- Docs (https://doc.oroinc.com/user/concept-guides/ai/, version 7.0): the Smart Agent "allows logged-in buyers
  to engage with the platform using **natural spoken or written language**"; "any logged-in buyer can access
  the Oro AI Smart Agent by clicking its icon in the storefront"; "integrated into the OroCommerce
  **Enterprise** storefront UI."
- **AI SmartOrder**: "Turn emailed PDFs into draft orders without manual entry"; docs: mailbox automation scans
  "incoming emails with attached purchase orders in JPG, PNG, or PDF format" and creates a draft order in the
  back office. Their time-saving and line-count figures (30 to 2 minutes, 700 lines in 30 seconds, 20% less
  entry at DiversiTech) are their own and stay OFF the page.
- OroIQ (staff, back office, "currently in customer beta"); MCP server for reps' own AI tools; semantic search;
  recommendations. AI is "included in your OroCommerce license" (Vertex AI recommendations excepted).
- Third party: an Alexa connector by Agence Dn'D was announced on Oro's own blog in **2017**
  (https://oroinc.com/b2b-ecommerce/blog/alexa-meets-orocommerce-use-alexa-connector-agence-dnd-fetch-orocommerce-data/),
  for fetching OroCommerce data. Old; do not present as current.
⇒ **Never "they type, we speak", never "the only voice option", never "Oro has no way to order by voice".**
The honest difference: SmartAgent lives inside the Enterprise storefront for a buyer who is logged in to it;
VoiceOrder Solutions is a separate phone app built only for the restaurant's reorder, set up per account from the
distributor's spreadsheet. Whether SmartAgent shows the buyer the order to check before placing it was NOT
verified: do not claim it does not.

## VOS + OroCommerce
No published connection (search 2026-11-09). VOS delivery paths: email (PDF/Excel/Word), EDI, API, QuickBooks.
An order sent through our app does **not** pass through the OroCommerce storefront, so it is not in that store's
order history, reorder lists or SmartAgent answers. Over EDI or the API it may reach the ERP or OroCommerce if
the distributor's setup takes it: a demo question. An order sent to QuickBooks is not in OroCommerce.

## Angle for the VOS page
**Built for complex buying; a kitchen reorder is short.** OroCommerce's own words sell complexity: corporate
accounts, account hierarchies, roles and permissions, approval chains, spend limits and budgets, RFQs and quote
negotiation, matrix and bulk CSV entry, millions of SKUs. A restaurant's reorder is the opposite: one chef, a
short list of the usual items, no approval, often after close. Keep OroCommerce for the accounts and orders
that need that machinery (and for the restaurants already happy in the store); give the chefs who would rather
call a short spoken order in a phone app built around their own guide, which they check before sending.
Oro's own AI page concedes the habit ("Many buyers still prefer sending orders or calling their rep instead of
navigating a portal"), and its answer, SmartAgent, sits inside the Enterprise storefront for logged-in buyers.
Distinct from: NetSuite (store add-on to license), Acumatica (kitchens skip the free portal), inFlow (Showroom
promised no more phone orders), Odoo (reorder last order vs today's shortages), Syspro (AI needs a written order),
B2B Wave (closest-job typing vs talking).

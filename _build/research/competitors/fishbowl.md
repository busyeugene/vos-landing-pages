## The QuickBooks trap
VOS's verified delivery formats include QuickBooks. On a Fishbowl setup, sales orders live in Fishbowl and
Fishbowl creates the QuickBooks invoice after shipping. Whether a VOS order put into QuickBooks would ever
reach Fishbowl depends on sync settings (two-way sync is advertised for QuickBooks Online, "bi-directional
sync" is "Coming soon" in the plan table). ⇒ Say QuickBooks is one of our formats, say a file, EDI or the API
is usually the more direct choice because the sales order starts in Fishbowl, and leave it to the demo.
Never imply a QuickBooks order lands in Fishbowl, and never use the canonical bullet "Over EDI, the API or
QuickBooks, nobody on your team retypes it" on this page (it is not true for a Fishbowl user's QuickBooks).

## Angle for the VOS page
Fishbowl's own picture of a distributor is retail: its distributor persona's morning opens with "EDI orders
from two retailers", its automatic order intake comes from online stores, marketplaces, retailer EDI and
B2B portals, and its promise is "Nothing typed twice" between the shelf and QuickBooks. A restaurant kitchen
sends none of those: the chef phones, and someone types the order once. VoiceOrder Solutions is built for the
restaurant order: the chef says it in an app, checks each line and sends it, and it reaches you as a file,
over EDI or by API. Fishbowl keeps stock, lots, picking and the QuickBooks sync. H1: "For Restaurant Orders"
("Built For ..." ran to 5 lines at 1440px). Distinct from the ERP pages (NetSuite: no extra module; Syspro: orders in writing for its AI;
Acumatica: kitchens skipping the portal; Business Central: check before sending).

**RE-ANGLED in the 2026-11-09 review** (the first angle leaned on "channels", which Cin7 owns, and on "in
writing", which collided with Syspro's H1 and final CTA). New angle, from the facts above: Fishbowl promises
"Nothing typed twice", and the one typing it leaves is the FIRST, the phoned order keyed in by hand. On a
Fishbowl setup the sales order must start in Fishbowl (the QuickBooks invoice follows it, and sales orders do
not pass automatically from QuickBooks), so that order has to be keyed into Fishbowl itself. H1 "Not A
Fishbowl Inventory Alternative: Fix The Phone Order". Honesty limit: by email the desk still enters the order
(from a list the chef checked); over EDI or the API it may go straight in, and the demo checks. The "retail
morning" persona stays as ONE problem card. The portal stays conceded in an FAQ (never name the third-party
portals on the page). Fishbowl's EDI is built for retailer trading partners; whether it takes restaurant
orders from us over EDI is a demo question (its own FAQ on the page).

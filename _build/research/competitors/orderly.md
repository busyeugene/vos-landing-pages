## ⚠ TRAP 0: "Orderly" is now "Back Office" by Buyers Edge Platform
Every getorderly.com page carries the banner **"Orderly is now Back Office!"** and "Login" goes to
`app.bepbackoffice.com`. bepbackoffice.com `/about-us/`: **"2012: Orderly is founded"**, **"2019: BEP
acquires Orderly"**, **"Buyers Edge Platform merges RASI, Orderly, and FoodBAM to create Back Office"**,
**"2023: Back Office is launched"**. The iOS app is now **"Back Office by BEP"** (developer "Orderly",
seller SIFTIT, INC., v3.9.41, 26 ratings, 3.4 avg). getorderly.com `/about` says core offerings and
pricing are unchanged and existing users keep their setup. So the page must say "Orderly (now Back
Office by Buyers Edge Platform)" once, early, or a searcher who knows the rebrand reads it as stale.
Buyers Edge Platform (BEP) is also a purchasing network: **"Managed alliance of 125+ independently
owned distributors."** and **"negotiated pricing on foodservice suppliers"** (`/about-us/`, `/faqs/`).

## 9. Real strengths a fair page must concede (biggest first)
1. **Food cost and COGS done for you, from invoices, without counts or data entry**, with a human
   team sending weekly recommendations. VOS does none of this.
2. **Invoice capture by photo, scan, EDI or supplier sync**, stored for the accountant. VOS: none.
3. **Ordering to all your vendors in one place, phone or desktop, with custom order guides** (+ par
   ordering and over-ordering alerts as a paid add-on). Overlaps VOS, and is broader in reach.
4. Recipe costing, usage/waste and AVT reporting, price-creep and above-market alerts, multi-unit comparisons.
5. AP / bill pay with QuickBooks Online; under Back Office, accounting and payroll too.
6. 27 named POS integrations; a buying network behind it (negotiated supplier pricing).
7. Published, month-to-month pricing; US-based support; hands-on onboarding.

## 10. Sentences that would be FALSE ("Orderly cannot / does not ...")
- ...take orders / let you order from your phone (app places supplier orders).
- ...have order guides or pars (custom order guides; Par-Based Ordering add-on).
- ...work with your existing suppliers (claims all vendors / all US suppliers).
- ...have a mobile app (iOS app: counts, invoices, orders).
- ...use automation / learn from your data (pattern alerts, auto-mapping; parent advertises AI Matching).
- ...connect to your POS or accounting (27 POS, QuickBooks Online).
- ...publish prices / lock you in (prices published, month-to-month).
- ...help you count inventory (optional counts in the app).
- Unverifiable either way, so never assert: how orders are transmitted; whether orders get a
  supplier-side number or confirmation; whether order-guide prices are live; whether ordering works offline.

## The page's angle (proposed)
**Orderly (now Back Office) is where you see what the food actually cost you, built from the invoice
after the truck arrives; VoiceOrder Solutions is only the step before, for the distributor that gave
you the app: you speak the order into that distributor's own guide, check each line, and send it with
an order number and timestamp.** Specific to Orderly: because its food cost runs off invoices, an
order placed in VoiceOrder Solutions still reaches Orderly's numbers the usual way, when that
delivery's invoice is snapped, scanned or synced (that is the invoice doing it, not any link
between the two products). Concede up front that Orderly's ordering reaches every vendor and
VoiceOrder Solutions reaches only distributors on it, so this is an add for one distributor, not a
second ordering hub. Caveat for the writer: for that distributor, Orderly's ordering-side extras
(par-based ordering, over-ordering alerts) would not see orders placed elsewhere; an FAQ should say so.

**Table rows, both columns tick (Orderly's strengths, kept):**
1. Food cost and COGS tracked from your invoices, without counts
2. Invoices captured by photo, scan or EDI
3. One screen to order from every vendor you buy from (Orderly's centralized ordering)

**Table rows, only the voice step differs:**
1. Speak the order to your distributor and review each line before sending (Orderly: "Not listed on their site")
2. Guide comes from the distributor itself: the items your account buys, at the price they set (Orderly: "Not listed on their site"; their prices update from the last invoice)
3. Each order to that distributor carries its order number and timestamp, any hour, and picks up where you left off if interrupted (Orderly: "Not listed on their site")

## Spelling (decides the registry `competitor`)
- The live marketing site writes the name **"Syspro"** in body copy and in every `<title>`
  ("ERP Software for Manufacturing & Distribution | Syspro"), after a rebrand announced in
  https://www.syspro.com/press_release/syspro-marks-a-breakthrough-year-with-ai-driven-platform-modernization-strategic-acquisitions-and-new-global-brand/
  ("Syspro has unveiled a renewed purpose and brand – 'Smarter. Faster. Built for Your Industry'").
- The logo, the older help site (help.syspro.com) and trade press still write "SYSPRO".
- The page follows the current site: **Syspro**. Keyword matching is case-insensitive, so
  `syspro alternative` is unaffected.

  "there is a cost associated as credit bundles must be purchased ... One credit is used each time a
  purchase order is uploaded and analyzed." Runs in Sales Order Entry / Sales Order Entry Express.
- Draft order review (sales order page, 2026): "Sales orders can now be held in draft status while the
  rules engine in Syspro Torque checks header and line details before release, so errors are caught at
  entry instead of in the warehouse."
- Syspro Torque (https://www.syspro.com/syspro-torque/, launched 2 Sept 2026 per
  https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/09/syspro-puts-ai-agents-to-work-inside-distributor-operations/):
  agents that "monitor, recommend and execute across procurement, inventory, customer service, planning
  and finance" (https://www.syspro.com/solutions/artificial-Intelligence/);
  **Re-checked 2026-11-09 (review): https://www.syspro.com/syspro-torque/ says "Syspro Torque is
  currently available through the Controlled Availability Program ahead of general availability".**
  ⇒ Every Torque / order-rules line on the page is scoped ("not yet generally available", "open so far
  only to a controlled set of existing users", "still in early release"); never present it as GA. "Ready-made agents handle
  common operational checks, from validating an order against every applicable rule"; "Operations teams
  describe what they need in plain English and Syspro Torque builds the agent". Controlled release,
  "participants are primarily existing Syspro customers", usage-based pricing (trade press).
- SIDEKICK: "The chat layer inside Syspro ... AI Knowledge Assistant"; with Torque "the full agent
  surface ... running under the operator's existing Syspro permissions". It serves STAFF.
⇒ The honest distinction: Syspro's AI READS an order the customer has already written down (a PO
  document) and its rules CHECK an order your team has entered. Neither helps the kitchen that never
  writes a PO and just calls. VoiceOrder Solutions is where the kitchen makes the order itself, by
  voice, and checks the lines before sending.

## ⚠ VOICE
- **Native:** nothing. No "voice" on any syspro.com page read (homepage, distribution, food, sales
  orders, portal, POS, mobile warehouse, warehouse, AI, Torque). Torque takes "plain English" from
  staff, which is typed instructions to build agents, not voice ordering.
- **Third-party voice ordering for Syspro:** web search (2026-11-09) found none named for Syspro.
  General voice/AI order-entry tools that post into many ERPs through an API do exist, and Syspro's
  API (`AddSalesOrder`, Harmony API, used by iPaaS vendors) makes that possible. ⇒ The page must not
  claim to be the only voice option for Syspro; one FAQ says other voice and AI order tools can post
  into Syspro, without naming them.

## Angle for the VOS page
- **Syspro works on orders that already exist in writing; VoiceOrder Solutions is for the orders
  nobody writes down.** Its AI reads a customer's purchase order (PDF or photo), its portal takes a
  typed order, and Torque checks the order after your team enters it. A kitchen ordering from a food
  distributor usually sends none of those: it calls, texts or leaves a voicemail. With VoiceOrder
  Solutions the kitchen says the order in the app and checks each line, so a written order exists
  before anything reaches Syspro.
- **Second strand:** Syspro's food page is about the plant (recipes, MOM, batch traceability). If you
  make food as well as move it, Syspro covers that and VoiceOrder Solutions has nothing to add there.
- **Concede first:** ledger, credit control, lot traceability and recalls, FEFO, warehouse and scanning,
  contract pricing and promotions, customer portal with order status and payment, AI that reads POs,
  rules checks on draft orders.

## Must NOT claim
- that Syspro has no customer ordering, no order history/status for customers, no AI, no EDI, no
  mobile; that Syspro is "for manufacturers only" or "not for distributors"; enterprise-only (it says
  mid-sized);
- that VoiceOrder Solutions is the only voice option for Syspro, or connects / integrates with Syspro;
- any Syspro price, the third-party estimates, the credit cost of PO reading, or a go-live duration
  for Syspro;
- that a spoken order cannot be wrong (the safeguard is the kitchen reviewing each line);
- any named partner (TrueCommerce, Stock2Shop, App4Sales) or any other ERP: claims.js bans several;
- that Syspro's own customer counts or efficiency figures ("30% increase in efficiency", "60+
  countries") are facts.

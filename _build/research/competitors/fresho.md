# Fresho (fresho.com) — competitor research for `/fresho-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-10-10** (homepage, /orderpilot, /pricing).

## ⚠⚠ THE PLAN'S PREMISE IS OUT OF DATE. READ THIS FIRST.
CLAUDE.md planned Fresho as "a supplier ordering app, the same SHAPE as VOS, not a platform". It
is now the opposite: a **full wholesale operations platform** plus an AI order-entry product.

Homepage title: **"Order & Operations Management System"**. The buyer is the supplier or
wholesaler ("Refer a supplier & earn up to $2,000").

Eugene's decision 2026-10-10: **fresh-food depth versus one step. "Add, not adopt."**

## Two products, and the distinction matters
**Fresho Operations** — "Run your entire wholesale operation in one platform for better efficiency
and visibility." Modules, their words: Online Ordering, Inventory Management, Pricing & Margins,
Delivery Management, Invoicing & Payments, Reporting, Messaging & Insights, Digital Picking &
Packing. Note "**Operations always includes online ordering**".

**Fresho OrderPilot** — "Connect OrderPilot to your existing systems and automate your order entry
at scale with the power of AI." H1: "Order automation software for food wholesalers."

## ⚠ TRAP 1: OrderPilot PARSES VOICEMAILS. Do not write "they type, we speak".
Verbatim: **"OrderPilot automatically turns emails, texts, WhatsApp messages, voicemails and PDFs
into structured sales orders 24/7."** Fourth competitor in this batch to touch voice after
WizCommerce, GrubMarket and NECS Entree. Grep for "voice" before arguing input method.

## ⚠ TRAP 2: OrderPilot is ADDITIVE, so "platform versus layer" is false here.
Their own words: **"Works with your existing ERP or system"**, and from the pricing FAQ: **"You can
use any combination of solutions, or use one or more alongside your existing system."** So Fresho
sells a layer too. The Pepper page's "a platform you move onto" argument **does not transfer** and
must not be reused on this page.

The honest form of Eugene's spine: Fresho sells **depth in fresh food**, right through to run
sheets, invoices and payments. VOS is **one step wide**. A wholesaler who needs delivery runs,
picking and invoicing should buy Fresho, and the page should say so plainly.

## ⚠ TRAP 3: their explicit promise is the inverse of ours. This is the sharpest honest contrast.
OrderPilot, on how it fits an operation: **"Customers continue ordering the same way they always
have."** That is a deliberate, sensible strategy: change nothing for the buyer, absorb the mess
with AI.

VOS's thesis is the opposite: the account's habit changes, so the order arrives already itemized
and confirmed. Both are legitimate and a buyer should choose knowingly. Frame it as a **strategy
choice, never as a flaw**, and keep it off the parse-versus-author axis, which belongs to the
WizCommerce page.

Also from the same list, quotable and fair: **"Accuracy improves over time as orders are refined
and corrected."** So there is a correction loop, by their own description.

## The fresh-food specialism is real
Resource guides are **Fruit & Veg Wholesale, Meat & Poultry Wholesale, Seafood Wholesale**, plus
"Fresh Food Insiders". Named customers on the OrderPilot page: **Prime Cut, MyButcher, GetFish**,
all fresh protein or seafood. Their quotes are about **run sheets and delivery runs**, which is
operational depth VOS has nothing to say about. Concede it generously.

## Pricing — ⚠ the MOST transparent of the twelve. Concede it outright.
- **"Tailored pricing starts from $499 monthly."**
- **"You pay a one-off set up fee, and a recurring monthly subscription based on average order
  volume and the solutions you need."**
- Two worked examples: a 1,000-orders-per-week food service business with an existing ERP using AI
  order entry on 20% of orders at **USD $1,230** monthly; a 400-orders-per-week seafood business on
  Operations with eCommerce at **USD $953** monthly.
- Region-specific pricing pages including **United States**, plus AU, NZ, UK & Ireland.
- A savings calculator: "Use your business data to see how much you could save with Fresho."

VOS publishes nothing, so Fresho wins the pricing row. **Keep their figures off the page** (stale
numbers are what a fact-checker catches); say they publish a starting price and worked examples,
and let the sourcing FAQ carry the date. Never pair any of it with a VOS figure.

Third page in this batch where "pricing is parity" fails, after OrderEase and REKKI.

## Unaudited numbers — do not repeat as results
"Save up to 6 hours per 200 orders", the Trustpilot "4.9 Excellent" badge, and the worked pricing
examples as if they were quotes for the reader. Customer quotes about run sheets are theirs.

## The angle for this page (must differ from all eleven before it)
> Fresho goes deep: ordering, stock, margins, delivery, picking, invoicing, payments, reporting,
> and an AI that reads the messages your customers already send. VOS goes narrow, and it changes
> what they send. One is an operations decision. The other is ten accounts next week.

Supports, all checkable:
1. **Depth versus width.** Their module list is the evidence, and it is genuinely strong in fresh
   categories. VOS has no run sheet, no invoice, no payment and no pick list, and says so.
2. **Preserve the habit, or change it.** Their published promise is that customers carry on exactly
   as before. VOS's whole value is that the ordering habit moves into the app and the buyer
   confirms the lines. Present as a fork in the road, not a defect.

**Concede generously and early:** the whole operations suite, delivery and last mile, invoicing and
payments, picking and packing, margins, reporting, the fresh-category depth, voicemail and WhatsApp
parsing, US pricing pages and published prices. Give Fresho the first rows of the table.

⚠ **Never imply Fresho is a rip-and-replace.** OrderPilot is explicitly additive and the pricing
FAQ says solutions can run alongside an existing system. The distinction is scope, not disruption.

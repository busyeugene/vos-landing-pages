## Angle for the VOS page
**Bring restaurants on board.** TrueCommerce's own pitch is onboarding "every supplier, no matter their
size or technical capability" and connecting trading partners so their orders arrive with no rekeying.
Every path it offers (integrated EDI, SFTP/AS2, a supplier portal, web EDI, a Shop storefront) is a
document, a file or a typed web order. The restaurant that still orders by phone is the account none of
those paths was built to bring in, so its calls are still keyed by your desk. VOS is the on-ramp for that
account: the distributor sends a spreadsheet, the chef downloads the app, says the order and checks each
item. TrueCommerce keeps the trading partners, suppliers, Shop, VMI and e-invoicing.
- Distinct from SPS (chains require EDI / independents never set it up): the argument here is about
  TrueCommerce's onboarding promise and who its paths are designed for, not about EDI compliance.
- Distinct from Acumatica ("kitchens that skip the portal"): Shop is conceded in full; the page does not
  argue that restaurants ignore it.

## Must NOT claim
- That TrueCommerce lacks customer ordering, reorder, quick order or 24/7 access (Shop has them).
- Anything about a Shop mobile app either way.
- Any TrueCommerce integration, partnership, network membership or joint support. They are separate
  products with no built-in link.
- That restaurants cannot do EDI; their unaudited numbers; a price; that VOS does VMI, forecasting,
  e-invoicing or supplier onboarding.
- "Their AI serves your staff" as an argument (reserved for other pages); TrueCommerce AI may be stated
  as a fact only.

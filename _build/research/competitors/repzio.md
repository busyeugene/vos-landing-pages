## Angle for the VOS page
RepZio is built to SELL A LINE: presentations, flipbooks, bestseller lists, barcode-scanned showrooms,
badge scanning and lead retrieval at the Atlanta, Las Vegas and High Point markets, "containers of
goods", and ShopZio for discovery. All of that exists to win a buyer's order for a line. A restaurant's
reorder needs none of it: the chef knows the items, and the order comes back every few days. With
RepZio alone that reorder is a call or text to the rep (who types it into the app) or a login to the
B2B Direct storefront. VoiceOrder Solutions gives that reorder its own way in: the chef says it from
their own guide and checks each item. H1 "Not A RepZio Alternative: Reorders Need No Pitch".
Distinct from: Pepperi ("the visit vs the reorder nobody attends": never argue visits or attendance),
Unleashed ("reps sell, chefs order / who enters it": the rep-types-the-call fact is one card, not the
lead), inFlow ("name items vs shop a catalog": never argue browsing), B2B Wave (typed portal vs voice).

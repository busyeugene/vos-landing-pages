## 4. ⚠ TRAP: voice / AI / scan search (every hit)
- **voice, voicemail, speak, dictate: ZERO hits** on every MarketMan page read, the App Store listing and the
  2025 release. A web search for MarketMan voice features found none. So voice is genuinely absent today.
- **BUT AI is everywhere, including on ordering.** Page title **"AI-Powered Inventory Management Software for
  Restaurants"**; **"AI-powered suggestions on what to order each week."** (pricing); partners page lists
  **"AI Ordering"** (Enterprise); App Store v14.2 (05/06/2025): **"New HQ dashboard Realtime scanning AI
  ordering."**; **"AI-powered Receiving, with real-time pricing"**; vs-MarginEdge **"AI Ordering Tools | Yes |
  Yes-grey"** and **"Powered by advanced, fully adaptive AI, MarketMan continues learning from every invoice
  processed"**.
- **Scan / camera / photo:** invoices only. **"simply snap a quick photo of your paper invoice."**, **"you can
  photograph it with the MarketMan mobile app or forward it by email."**, **"uses OCR technology to scan and
  extract data from paper or digital invoices."** (AP page); App Store **"Realtime OCR"**. AI Recipe Match
  **"leverages advanced image recognition technology to automatically read and interpret uploaded ingredient
  lists"** (2025 release). No barcode counting found.
- ⚠ Consequences for copy: never "MarketMan has no AI" or "they type, we speak" as a smart-vs-dumb contrast.
  Their AI decides WHAT to order (suggestions, forecasts); the order itself is approved with a click. The
  honest contrast is the input to one distributor (spoken, from that distributor's guide) vs a suggested PO
  you approve. Voice is absent today, and the sourcing FAQ should date that statement.

## ⚠ TRAP: the products do not connect, and here that has a practical cost
MarketMan's receiving, price tracking and inventory update hang off a PO created in MarketMan. An order spoken
into VOS creates no MarketMan PO. The page must not imply the VOS order shows up in MarketMan. The safe
honest line: the invoice still reaches MarketMan the way it does today (photo or email scan); the VOS order
carries its own number and timestamp on the distributor's side. Do not claim what MarketMan does with an
invoice that has no matching PO (unverified).

## Proposed angle
**MarketMan works out what your kitchen should buy and what it cost you afterwards; when one of your
distributors runs VOS, the order to that distributor can be spoken in their app, from the guide and prices
they set for your account, and reviewed line by line before it goes.** Keep MarketMan for counts, pars,
suggestions, invoices and costing; add VOS for the one call-or-portal step with a VOS distributor. Honest
corollary: if a distributor is already two-way integrated with MarketMan, VOS adds little for that supplier;
say so (it reads as the most trustworthy line on the page).

### Candidate table rows: both tick (`MarketMan + VoiceOrder Solutions` vs `MarketMan on its own`)
1. Inventory counts, par levels and suggested orders (both: MarketMan does it, it stays).
2. Invoice scanning and food-cost tracking (both).
3. Order from a phone app (both: MarketMan web/mobile; VOS app).

### Candidate rows where only VOS's ordering job differs
1. Speak the order to a distributor on VOS, and review each line before sending (VOS tick; MarketMan: text
   cell "Not listed on their site" for voice ordering, dated).
2. The order list is the distributor's own guide for your account, at the price they set (VOS tick; MarketMan
   integrated suppliers push prices and items too, so the MarketMan cell must be fair: "For integrated
   suppliers" rather than a dash).
3. The distributor gives you the app; no software subscription of your own for that ordering step
   (careful: KB says cost questions go to the distributor; phrase as "Comes through your distributor" vs
   "Your own subscription"), or alternatively "Order number and timestamp from the distributor's side".

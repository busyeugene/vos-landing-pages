## ⚠ EDI, email, fax, AI, voice

## Angle for the VOS page
Sana's own pitch is the direction of the information: it "closes the digital distance, the gap between what
the business knows and what the buyer sees", and its homepage asks "Do your buyers still call to check prices
or stock?". It ends the calls that ASK, by showing each customer your ERP. The call it leaves is the one that
TELLS: a chef reading out what the kitchen needs. Sana's answer to that chef is to log in, use an order
template or quick order and check out; the chefs who will not keep calling, and your team enters the call in
your ERP (Sana's own food-distributor story: staff "training customers on using the web-shop", some needing
"more onboarding than others"). VoiceOrder Solutions takes that call's job: the chef SAYS the order in an app,
checks each item and sends it. H1: "Not A Sana Commerce Alternative: Chefs Say What They Need".
Distinct from: Acumatica (kitchens that skip a free portal), inFlow (Showroom's "no more phone orders"),
NetSuite (store is an add-on), Odoo (Order Again), Brightpearl (accuracy before automation).

## Must NOT claim
- Never name the two ERP families (claims.js) or Sana's partners; write "your ERP".
- Never "Sana can't take phone orders" / "no mobile ordering" / "not for food" / "not for small businesses".
- Never that our order passes through Sana, appears as a Sana web order, or that Sana can receive it. Once
  entered in your ERP it is an ERP order; Sana says its account pages list orders "directly from the ERP" too:
  attribute it if used.
- Never that the chef sees live stock or back-in-stock dates in OUR app (Sana shows them on its store).
- Never that our prices sync from the ERP: "You keep each guide's prices current in our admin; whether they can
  update from your ERP is a question for the demo."
- Sana's web orders reach the ERP with nobody retyping; our emailed orders are still entered by the desk. Say
  so; keep accounts already ordering on Sana on Sana.
- None of Sana's percentages, order counts or case-study numbers. No Sana price.

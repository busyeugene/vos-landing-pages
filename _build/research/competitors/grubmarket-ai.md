## The angle for this page (must differ from Pepper, Choco and Burnt)
This is the closest competitor to VOS so far, because GrubAssist is also **voice-powered and
mobile**. The honest distinction is **whose hands the voice is in**:
- Every one of GrubAssist's own use-case tags is inward facing: business analysis, performance,
  cash flow, anomalies. It is an assistant for **the distributor's own team**, which can also
  create an order while it is at it.
- VOS puts the microphone in **the customer's** hands. The order is built and approved by the
  kitchen, so there is no order for your team to create at all.
- Their separate **order extraction** product reads voicemails, emails and texts, so inbound
  parsing is covered on their side. VOS does not parse anything; it replaces the message.
- Second real distinction: **there is something to train.** Their AI Training Module exists so you
  can tailor the assistant. On VOS the order guide is the whole configuration, and nothing is
  trained.

**Concede plainly:** GrubAssist answers questions about the business by voice and VOS cannot do
that at all. Two table rows go to them, and an FAQ says so.

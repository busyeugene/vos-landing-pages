# SPS Commerce (spscommerce.com) — competitor research for `/alternatives/sps-commerce-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-11-09.** Pages read directly and through the r.jina.ai reader (same page, as text).
Batch 2, wave 3 (webshops / EDI / order capture). No prior entry exists.

## Positioning (verbatim)
- Homepage (https://www.spscommerce.com/): **"The Intelligent Supply Chain Network"** /
  "Smarter Connections for Stronger Supply Chains"; "we power the connections that keep commerce moving
  forward". Network figures on the homepage and the Fulfillment page (46,000+ subscribing customers,
  300,000+ trading relationships, 750M+ transactions a year, 400+ integration partners) are their own
  unaudited numbers: **never repeat them**, not even as paraphrase ("tens of thousands").
- What is EDI (https://www.spscommerce.com/what-is-edi/): **"SPS is the only full-service EDI solution on
  the market."** (their claim; do not repeat). EDI "is how retailers and suppliers exchange critical business
  documents—like purchase orders, invoices, and shipping notices—without email or manual entry."
- How EDI works, their own five steps (same page): **"1. Your business system generates a document (like a
  purchase order) 2. The system converts it into a standardized EDI format 3. It's transmitted securely to
  your trading partner's system 4. Their system receives and processes it automatically 5. They respond with
  their own EDI document (confirmation, shipment notice, invoice)"**. ⇒ EDI starts in the CUSTOMER's
  business system. (Do not build the page on "a written order": Syspro owns that.)
- Footer/about: "The industry's most broadly adopted retail cloud services platform"; the partner
  directory is titled "Connect to the Largest Retail Network" / "Retailer & Trading Partner List".

  The foodservice story on the page is the SUPPLIER side of large distributors, plus grocers.

## What it does for a distributor (concede all of it)
- EDI with trading partners that require it: orders (850), acknowledgments, ship notices (ASN/856),
  invoices (810); "Fulfillment automates the handoffs, acknowledgments, ship notices, and invoices"
  (Fulfillment page). Compliance with each retailer's rules; label printing (Web Fulfillment shipping
  documentation, definitions page below).
- **Connects into your ERP:** "Fulfillment extends beyond SPS itself, securely into the ERP you already
  run ... in addition to the 3PLs and warehouses fulfilling on your behalf"; FAQ **"Does Fulfillment
  replace our ERP or existing systems? No."** "It handles the trading partner side of the connection".
  The same FAQ names ERPs that claims.js bans: never repeat the names.
- **EDI AND API:** "Most first-time suppliers are compliant within 24 hours, whether the connection runs
  over EDI, API, or another format" (Fulfillment FAQ). ⚠ Their own fast-setup figure: never set our 24-48
  hours against it, and never claim we are faster to set up.
- Two ways to run it (definitions page,
  https://www.spscommerce.com/customer-resources/fulfillment-definitions-additional-feature-pricing/):
  **Integrated Fulfillment** (documents mapped into your system, end-to-end testing per trading partner)
  and **Web Fulfillment** ("account access to SPS Commerce Web Fulfillment; Capabilities for processing,
  viewing, downloading or printing all trading partner requirements"). ⇒ On Web Fulfillment, a
  distributor may still key a chain's order into its own system; do not build on this, it is a detail.
- **MAX** (https://www.spscommerce.com/products/max-ai/), their agentic AI: "watches orders, shipments,
  and invoices, answers questions from real trading-partner behavior, and flags risk before it becomes a
  chargeback. MAX does the watching. You review and approve." ⇒ AI for the supplier's team on EDI
  traffic. "Their AI serves your staff" is reserved for sage-x3 / prophet-21: mention MAX at most once,
  as a fact, not as the argument.
- Also sells: Analytics/Decision Intelligence, Assortment (item data), Revenue Recovery (deductions),
  E-Invoicing, EDI Testing and Certification, Supply Chain Performance Suite, SPS for 3PLs.

  "Access digital catalogs and simplified ordering across your supplier base"; add-on **"In-Aisle

## The connection path (us and them)
- **VOS + SPS: no published connection, no partnership.** VoiceOrder Solutions can deliver an order as

## Angle for the VOS page
- **SPS exists because a customer REQUIRES EDI.** Its whole pitch is compliance with each trading
  partner's rules ("Most retailers require it"; "already knows these rules, for every retailer";
  chargebacks when you miss one), set up one partner at a time. The accounts that require EDI are the large
  ones: retailers, grocers, big distributors. **Your independent restaurant accounts require nothing:**
  no rulebook, no EDI system to generate a purchase order, so they phone, text or leave a voicemail, and
  SPS has nothing to connect to at their end. VoiceOrder Solutions is for those accounts: the chef speaks
  the order in our app and checks each item.
- Distinct from Cin7 (which owns "the channel Cin7 does not connect / retail chains get EDI, restaurants
  phone"): this page argues from SPS's OWN mechanism, the trading partner and its rules. A chain hands you
  its rules and fines you in chargebacks when you miss them; a restaurant hands you a phone call. H1:
  "For Non-EDI Accounts". Keep "rules" language for the chain side only.
- Secondary strand: SPS's own buyer-side product (Order Central) is built for stores ordering by catalog
  and in-aisle scanning; a chef at the walk-in is a different job. One FAQ.
- Concede: EDI with chains and big partners, ASNs, invoices, labels, compliance, MAX, ERP connection,
  API connections, traceability, analytics, deductions. Ticks in both columns.

## Must NOT claim
- That our orders travel over SPS, that we are on the SPS network, that SPS can receive or translate our
  orders, any partnership, certification or joint support.
- That restaurants cannot do EDI (say most independents do not), or that SPS has no ordering product
  (Order Central exists, for retailers).
- Any SPS number (network size, 24-hour compliance, chargeback or OTIF percentages, customer counts,
  fees). The 24-hour compliance claim is theirs; never set our setup time against it.
- Names of the ERPs or retailers on their pages (claims.js bans several, e.g. the big ERPs and
  broadline distributors).
- That VoiceOrder Solutions sends ASNs, invoices or labels, or handles chargebacks, compliance or
  traceability.


## Re-check 2026-11-09 (wave-3 review, factcheck-b H1; re-read live the same day)
- https://www.spscommerce.com/blog/pdf-order-automation/ , title "How to achieve order automation – even
  when customers don't use EDI", "Last Updated August 6, 2025". Product: **"SPS Commerce PDF Order
  Automation"**. Verbatim: "Our PDF Order Automation uses your existing Fulfillment integrations to map and
  translate non-EDI orders directly into your EDI workflow"; "SPS PDF Order Automation is not OCR. Our
  solution uses AI"; the problem it answers: "But what about your customers who aren't set up to use EDI?"
  No mention of restaurants, phone or voice orders. (The reviewer also found it referenced on SPS's Shopify
  Fulfillment page and in a Jan 2026 BusinessWire release; not re-read here.)
- So the line above ("No email/PDF order capture product") was wrong, and the one-line concession "other
  companies sell AI that reads emailed orders" read as "not SPS". The page now concedes it by name in the
  Order Central FAQ: a chef who phones sends no PDF; whether it could read the PDF we email is a question
  for SPS Commerce; we built nothing for it and make no claim that it does. The sourcing FAQ lists the
  article. Never claim it reads our PDFs, or that it cannot.
- Also from the same review (factcheck-b L3): per-partner "map and trading partner testing" is a priced item
  for Integrated Fulfillment only, and the Fulfillment page says "the network already holds the map". The
  page now says each new trading partner "is set up and tested", not "mapped and tested".

## 3. ⚠ PLAN SCOPE: ordering is listed in the top plan only (the page's angle rests on this)
Pricing page, 2026-11-09: four plans. Essential and Plus show a monthly price; Growth and Advanced say "Talk to
Sales". Per-plan "Key features included" lists (raw capture, in order):
- plan 1: Guided Setup, Integration, Reporting and Business Intelligence
- plan 2: + Intranet, Checklist and HACCP Food Safety, Banking and Cash Control
- plan 3 ("Popular Plan"): + HR and Applicant Tracking, Employee Scheduling, Timekeeping and Payroll, Benefits
- plan 4 (Advanced): + **Inventory, Ordering and Receiving**, Transfers, Variance, Prep, Production Guide
So inventory and supplier ordering appear ONLY in the Advanced plan's list. Safe wording on the page: "On
Altametrics' pricing page, ordering and receiving are listed in the Advanced plan." Never say a given reader's
plan lacks it, never say they must upgrade, never price it. Zip Ordering may be sold separately: not
verified, so never say "Altametrics ordering costs more" or "only on Advanced" as an absolute.

## 4. ⚠ Voice / AI / phone search
- **Voice:** the only product voice is the AI time clock: **"Voice alerts and live feedback for employees"**
  (homepage). Their blog has articles on guest voice ordering (drive-thru, phone AI) and an explainer page on
  "AI ordering systems" for GUEST orders (phone, web, kiosk, drive-thru). These are articles, not a product
  page we could find. No supply ordering by voice found anywhere.
- **AI everywhere:** AI Forecasting, AI Workforce, AI Inventory ("AI-assisted counts"), AI Accounting, AI
  applicant screening, "A.I." as one of five suggested-order methods. Never "no AI", never "they type, we speak"
  as a smart-vs-dumb contrast.
- **Phone:** Zip Ordering sells "Eliminate Calling In Your Orders". So never claim Altametrics leaves you on the
  phone; say instead what happens with a distributor not linked to it, or when ordering is not in your plan.
- Guest-ordering confusion risk: Altametrics writes about guest voice ordering and drive-thru. The page says
  plainly it is about the supply order to your distributor, not guest orders.

## 8. Real strengths a fair page must concede (biggest first)
1. A whole restaurant operating system: labor scheduling, time clock, payroll, HR, cash office, food safety,
   reporting, accounting links. VoiceOrder Solutions does none of it.
2. Forecasting: sales and labor forecasts that also drive prep, production guides and order suggestions.
3. Ordering, where the plan includes it: five suggested-order methods, electronic orders straight into a
   linked distributor's system, delivery calendar, distributor item catalog, receiving, credits, substitutions.
4. Inventory: AI-assisted counts by storage area, actual vs theoretical, waste, transfers, variance.
5. Multi-unit and franchise reporting; mobile apps for iPhone and Android.

## 9. Sentences that would be FALSE
- "Altametrics has no ordering / cannot send orders to suppliers" (Automated Ordering, Electronic Ordering,
  Zip Ordering).
- "Altametrics has no mobile app" (iOS + Android).
- "Altametrics has no AI" (AI is in every module name).
- "Altametrics is only for chains" (their own FAQ says single restaurants too).
- "Altametrics leaves you phoning orders" (Zip: "Eliminate Calling In Your Orders").
- "Altametrics orders reach only big distributors" (Zip: "Local vendors can be easily added too!").
- True and checkable: no voice ordering of supplies found; ordering and receiving listed in the Advanced plan.

## Angle for the VOS page
**Altametrics is the operating system for the whole restaurant (shifts, payroll, cash, food safety, reports,
and, in its Advanced plan, counts and ordering); its own site calls suggested ordering "one part of the
overall operating picture". VoiceOrder Solutions is only the supply order itself: said on your phone to a
distributor that offers our app, checked line by line, sent with a number.** Two practical cases: a plan
that runs labor and cash but not ordering (the produce order still goes by phone), and a distributor not
linked to Altametrics' electronic ordering. Franchise note: a brand's ordering rules come first.
Distinct from the other operator pages: not "skip the call" (MarketMan), not "it plans, we place"
(Restaurant365), not approvals (Craftable), not "plan there" (Apicbase).

## Must NOT claim
- That the reader's plan lacks ordering, that they must upgrade, or any price.
- That Altametrics cannot order, has no app, has no AI, or is only for chains.
- That we connect to Altametrics, its supplier gateways or its POS links; that a spoken order shows up in
  Altametrics receiving, credits or food cost.
- That we suggest quantities, count stock, forecast, or set pars.
- Any of their location, worker, savings or time numbers.
- Any named distributor, POS or brand (claims.js bans; their customers' brand names stay off too).

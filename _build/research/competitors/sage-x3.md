# Sage X3 (sage.com) — competitor research for `/alternatives/sage-x3-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-11-09.** sage.com returns HTTP 403 to direct fetches, so every sage.com page
below was read through the r.jina.ai reader (same page, rendered to text). No prior entry exists.
Only Sage X3 facts are used. Sage 100, Sage 300, Sage Intacct and Sage 50 are different products
and sit in the same nav menu: nothing from their pages is carried over.

## What it does for a distributor (concede all of it)
- Distribution page: "real-time visibility into inventory, margins, and fulfilment"; "Fully integrated
  distribution functionalities"; "Global sourcing, importing and exporting, and inventory management";
  "Manage sales force assignments with ease and track corresponding targets and commissions."
- Supply chain page (https://www.sage.com/en-us/sage-business-cloud/sage-x3/product-capabilities/supply-chain-management/):
  purchasing ("RFQs ... purchase orders", "Multi-level signature management"), inventory ("Product data
  and multiple units of measure", "Quality control and sampling", "Replenishment and inter-site
  transfers", "Licence Plate Number (LPN)"), **sales: "Quotes, contracts and open orders",
  "Multi-level credit checking and order entry", "Order preparation, delivery, packing and shipping",
  "Inventory enquiries and allocations"**, and **customer service: "Customer call centre support"**.
  Planning: "Use forecasting and demand planning to reduce stock-outs".

  "Native multi-site, multi-currency, and multi-country management".
- ⇒ X3 is a full stock system of record with WMS, purchasing and planning. VoiceOrder Solutions is
  none of those; never imply it sees or plans stock better.

  https://www.sage.com/en-us/sage-business-cloud/sage-x3/product-capabilities/, supply chain,

- Capabilities overview: "you can extend the platform further through Sage X3 add-ons and certified
  partner solutions available in the Sage marketplace."
- Commercebuild (https://commercebuild.com/blog/the-power-of-a-sage-x3-or-300-ecommerce-customer-portal-integration/):

  easily replicate a previous order"), "Customer Portal ... built in 10-days or less", "Our solutions
  can be live in 30-days". Other partner portals/webstores exist (Experlogix, E-Link, Aphix, Net at
  Work, per search 2026-11-09).
- ⇒ Never write "Sage X3 customers cannot order online" or "a portal takes months": partner portals
  exist and claim days to weeks. The honest line: on X3 itself, ordering is entered by your staff;
  self-service ordering is a marketplace add-on, typed and searched. Keep partner names and their
  timelines off the page.

## ⚠⚠ VOICE: none native for ordering; a partner already sells REP-side voice for X3
- Native: "voice", "speech" and "spoken" appear on no X3 page read. Copilot is described as
  conversational natural-language chat ("embedded natural language chat to query the system"), not

- **Workd** (https://www.workd.com/integrations/sage-x3/): "Your team talks to their Workd agent the

  notes back"; "without changing your X3 configuration"; "Most teams are live in two to four weeks";
  "Distributors from $5M to $1.5B". It is for the distributor's TEAM, not its customers.
- Greytrix "Quick Order Entry" and Mysoft "Advanced Telesales" speed up STAFF order entry (not voice).
- ⇒ Never "the only voice option for Sage X3" or "nobody offers voice for X3". The defensible
  difference: existing voice tools for X3 are for your staff; VoiceOrder Solutions puts the voice
  app in the CUSTOMER's hands with line review before sending. Concede in an FAQ without naming them.

## Angle for the VOS page
- **Sage X3 is shaped to each business by a Sage partner**: lots, batches, recalls, allergens,
  quality, credit checks at order entry, the warehouse, purchasing. Concede all of it in the table.
- **Its order tools point inward**: a sales team with mobile access to customers, stock, past
  purchases and promotions; "customer call centre support"; "credit checking and order entry". The
  kitchen's own way to order is not one of X3's listed capabilities; it comes from the marketplace
  as a typed portal or a partner build.
- **VoiceOrder Solutions adds the kitchen's side without reopening the X3 setup**: no change to X3's
  configuration, workflows or screens to start (orders can arrive as email files); kitchens speak the
  order and check each line. Distinct from NECS Entree (typed vs spoken) and NetSuite (where the order
  starts; web store add-on; AI for staff): this page is about the X3 project and its partner.
- H1 direction: "Not A Sage X3 Alternative: Your Setup Stays Put".

## Must NOT claim
- That X3 has no customer ordering of any kind, no online reorder, or that a portal takes months.
- That X3 has no AI, no order automation, or no mobile (sales teams have mobile access).
- "The only voice option for Sage X3" / "nobody offers voice for X3" (Workd sells rep-side voice).
- Any Sage X3 integration, marketplace listing, certification or partnership for VoiceOrder Solutions.
- That X3 is not for food, not for distributors, or enterprise-only.
- Any price, the "50 working days" timeline as a knock, "75% of orders", "50%", "40%", or customer counts.
- That VoiceOrder Solutions plans, counts, traces or sees stock better than X3.
- Anything from Sage 100 / 300 / Intacct / 50.

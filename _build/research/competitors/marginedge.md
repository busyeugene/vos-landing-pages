## 3. Ordering in detail (the overlap)
**Ordering exists, is real, and is mobile.** Do not write it up as absent or minor in a way that
implies they lack it.
- /inventory-management H1: **"Inventory & ordering management"**. Section "ORDERING": **"Ordering and
  on-hand counts, side by side"**.
- **"Whether you're using a desktop or a smartphone, ordering for your restaurant has never been
  easier with order guides, EDI integration and inventory counts all in one place."**
- **"New vendor items are automatically added to existing guides (and can always be customized!)"**
- **"Easily set and see pars, on-hand and last count amounts for specific products on order guides
  (so no more flipping back and forth to determine order quantities)"**
- **"Products and prices are automatically updated on your sheets as you order"**
- **"Multi-units can centrally manage guides for some or all locations"**; commissary: **"Email
  automation simplifies communication between the ordering and commissary kitchens to track order
  status"**.
- /how-it-works: **"A centralized solution for ordering"**, **"Place orders through vendors right
  from MarginEdge."**, **"Easily set pars and on-hand levels"**.
- Mobile: Q1 2024 release notes, **"Ordering is now available from our mobile app!"** and **"You can
  conveniently place orders from anywhere, whether you're stocking the walk-in, watching a movie at
  the drive-in."**

**How an order is built** *[help snippets]*: an Order Guide is **"generated automatically"** for every
vendor whose invoices are processed in MarginEdge (so the guide's items and prices come from past
invoices: our inference from that plus **"Ingredient prices auto-update"** as invoices are processed,
/menu-analysis). Pars can be **"One Par each vendor item, or Daily Par"** by weekday; **"When placing
an order, your par will automatically populate"**; enter on-hand and **"a suggested order amount will
be calculated in the 'Qty' column."** So: par minus on-hand gives a suggested quantity the user edits.
- Web: Orders > Place New Order > pick vendor > fill the guide > review summary > Send. Mobile: "More"
  > "Place an Order". **"You cannot set up a vendor on your mobile device"**. Offline send: the app
  notifies when the order uploads or fails.
- Sales forecast does NOT generate order quantities: /sales-forecast says it informs **"staffing, prep,
  and purchasing volumes"**, nothing about suggested orders. Tom gives **"guidance on prep, ordering,
  and waste"** (/ai-for-restaurants). Do not credit them with forecast-driven ordering, and do not deny
  that Tom talks about ordering.

**How it reaches the supplier** *[help snippets]*: **email**, to whoever the restaurant enters (a rep
or an ordering inbox): **"Anyone who will accept orders via email can be setup in MarginEdge"**; turned
on per vendor with the checkbox **"Will you be placing orders via MarginEdge?"**. EDI ordering: **"MarginEdge
currently only supports EDI ordering for the vendor Profish. All other vendors will need to be setup
via email."** (undated; the marketing page says "EDI integration" generically, so treat Profish-only as
unconfirmed and keep it off the page).
- ⚠ **TRAP: "works with any supplier" is THEIR win, not ours.** MarginEdge ordering works with any
  vendor that takes email. VOS ordering works only with distributors who are on VOS. Concede it.
- ⚠ **TRAP: they DO connect to distributors**, for invoices. Homepage FAQ names **Gordon Food Service,
  Ben E. Keith, Shamrock and US Foods**; invoices arrive **"through EDI integrations"** (/how-it-works);
  H1 2026 notes add **"Gordon Foods Bid Export: Export your product list to GFS so they can bid on your
  purchases."** Never write "MarginEdge has no link to your distributor".
- The order is set up and keyed by the restaurant (its own vendor list, its own guide). The
  distributor is not a party to the setup. That is the honest structural difference from VOS.

## 9. Real strengths a fair page must concede (biggest first)
1. A whole restaurant back office: invoices, food cost, daily P&L, recipes, inventory, bill pay,
   accounting export. VOS does none of it.
2. Invoice capture by photo, email, upload or EDI, coded by AI plus people.
3. Ordering to ANY supplier that accepts email, from one guide per vendor, on web and mobile.
4. Pars (daily pars too) and on-hand counts on the guide, with a suggested quantity.
5. Published, flat, per-location price with no contract on monthly.
6. Price alerts on vendor items, theoretical vs actual usage, sales forecasting, Tom.
7. Multi-unit: central guides, transfers, commissaries.
8. Very wide POS and accounting coverage; distributor invoice feeds.

## 10. Sentences that would be FALSE ("MarginEdge cannot / does not ...")
- ...let you order from suppliers / order on a phone / use par levels / suggest a quantity: all FALSE.
- ...connect to distributors: FALSE (invoice EDI with GFS, BEK, Shamrock, US Foods; GFS bid export).
- ...use AI: FALSE. ...use the phone camera: FALSE (invoice photos, QR scan).
- ...publish pricing: FALSE. ...work across locations: FALSE.
- ...let you ask questions in plain language: FALSE (Tom; AI Connector).
- Safe and true as of today: no voice order taking appears anywhere; the order goes out as an email
  (EDI for named vendor(s) only, unconfirmed); it carries no distributor-side order number we can
  see; the guide is built from the restaurant's past invoices, not supplied by the distributor.
  Phrase these as what VOS does, not as what MarginEdge lacks.

## Proposed angle
**MarginEdge works on the invoice after the truck has been; VOS is the order before it: keep
MarginEdge as your back office, and if your distributor is on VOS, speak that distributor's order
into the app from the guide and prices it set up for you.** Distinct from xtraCHEF / R365 / MarketMan
because MarginEdge's own pitch is the invoice (photo in, coded in 24-48 hours), so the before/after
split is theirs to agree with, not ours to impose.

Table rows where BOTH tick (MarginEdge + VOS column and MarginEdge-alone column):
1. Place an order from your phone.
2. Order from a guide of the items you buy from that supplier.
3. Review the order before it is sent.

Rows where only the VOS ordering job differs:
1. Speak the order instead of keying quantities line by line.
2. The guide and prices come from your distributor, not rebuilt from last week's invoices.
3. Each order gets an order number and a timestamp from your distributor's side (orders placed in VOS).

Concede in the table or FAQ: works with any email-ordering supplier (MarginEdge), VOS only with
distributors on VOS; MarginEdge publishes its price.

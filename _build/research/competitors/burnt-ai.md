# Burnt (getburnt.ai) — competitor research for `/burnt-ai-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-10-10.** Client-approved competitor (2026-07-17). The product has **widened
since the July notes**: it is no longer just order intake, it is a multi-agent platform.

## Positioning (verbatim)
- H1: **"Automate food distribution"**
- Subhead: **"Deploy AI agents across teams to eliminate manual work and scale without adding
  headcount."**
- Self-description: "the AI watchtower for food distributors around the world"
- Named verticals: "seafood distribution, spice trading and restaurant supply chains"

## The agents (this is the shape that changed)
| Agent | Their words |
|---|---|
| Sales & operations | "Automate order intake and sales workflows across every channel with AI precision." It "validates your orders, increasing accuracy and preventing errors." |
| Procurement | "Smart procurement that keeps inventory stocked while optimizing costs and supplier relationships." Automates purchasing decisions and supplier communications. |

Platform framing: "Unlike isolated systems, our agents work together to power your whole business",
and "start with one agent or deploy the full platform".

## Order intake detail, from /product/sales-operations (verbatim)
- Channels read: **"Email (PDFs, inline emails, attachments)", "Phone and voicemail (via
  transcription)", "WhatsApp and SMS", "EDI", "Customer portals and spreadsheets", "Sales rep

- Review model: **"It uses confidence thresholds to determine when actions can be automated and
  when human review is required."** Exceptions are "automatically routed to the right teams

- "Automatically confirms orders, provides updates and answers common questions."
- ⚠ **"The page doesn't say whether customers review orders before posting."** That is the exact
  VOS distinction, and it is a statement about their documentation, which is checkable.

99.99% accuracy, 80% less admin time, 20% profit uplift. The July notes also recorded

## The angle for this page (must differ from Pepper and Choco)
Burnt's own pitch is **agents across the back office**: intake, purchasing, and chasing overdue
invoices by phone. That is a far wider remit than ordering.
- The honest contrast is **how much of your operation you are handing to software, and who is in
  the loop.** Burnt decides by confidence threshold when a human should look; VOS puts the check
  with the customer who built the order, every time, before it is sent.
- **On-site onboarding** is the other real one: their own page says their team works on site to
  configure the agents. VOS needs nobody on site and no agents to configure.
- Rollout time is a **weak** lead here (1 to 2 weeks is quick). Use it only as a table row, and
  state their range fairly. Do not make it the argument the way the Pepper page does.
- Concede clearly: Burnt reads more inbound channels than VOS does, including EDI and spreadsheets.
  VOS does not parse inbound channels at all; it replaces the message with a structured order.

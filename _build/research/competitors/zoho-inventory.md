# Zoho Inventory (zoho.com/inventory) — competitor research for `/alternatives/zoho-inventory-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-11-09.** zoho.com answered direct fetches; long pages were also read through the
r.jina.ai reader (same page, rendered to text). The US pages (`zoho.com/us/inventory/...`) are the ones
the global homepage links to. No prior entry exists. Batch 2 wave 2 (inventory systems).

## Positioning (verbatim)
- Homepage (https://www.zoho.com/inventory/): H1 **"Manage inventory and fulfill orders - the right way"**;
  "Automate your inventory operations and sell more with less effort."; "Everything you need from sales to
  shipping". No audience statement.
- Industry pages in the site nav: **Retail, Ecommerce, Manufacturing, Dropshipping**
  (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/inventory-software-for-retail/ etc.). No food, wholesale or
  distribution industry page. "Wholesale" appears only as a free "Wholesale Price Calculator" tool.
- Customers page (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/customers/): an industry filter includes "Food &
  Beverage"; stories include a seafood business (AZ Gems) and a winery (Byington Vineyard). ⇒ It HAS food
  customers. Never write "not built for food"; at most "its industry pages list retail, ecommerce,
  manufacturing and dropshipping" (breadth, not absence). The page does not use this.
- Part of the Zoho suite. Footer "Other Related Zoho Finance Apps": Zoho Books, Zoho Billing, Zoho
  Commerce, Zoho Expense. Feature list (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/kb/general-overview/zom-feature-list.html):
  **"Seamlessly integrate with our accounting platform - Zoho Books."**; **"Bring in your network of
  customers and suppliers from Zoho CRM."**

## What it does for a distributor (concede all of it)
- Multi-warehouse (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/multi-warehouse-stock/): "Manage stock across multiple
  storehouses"; "Synchronize sales across multiple warehouses and manage drop shipments, inter-warehouse
  transfers, and back orders with ease."
- Batch / expiry (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/serial-batch-tracking/): **"You can monitor expiration
  dates, conduct quality checks, and trace each product back to its batch. This enables you to handle
  warranty claims and product recalls with ease."** Feature list: "Batch Tracking - To track items based on
  batch numbers and expiry date."
- Reorders and buying: "Manage reorders, create purchase orders, record deliveries to your warehouse using
  purchase receives" (feature list).
- Order management (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/order-management/): "Generate sales orders, apply
  discounts, attach documents, and confirm orders"; backorders, partial fulfilment, dropshipping, approval
  workflows, returns, credit notes; picklists, packing and shipping.
- Per-customer prices: "Customize your item prices by creating price lists and assign them to your favorite
  customers, sales orders and invoices." (feature list); "How to associate a price list to a customer?"
  (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/kb/price-lists/pl-customer.html). Price lists export as .XLS or .CSV
  (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/kb/price-lists/pl-import.html).
- Invoices and payments; accounting sync. QuickBooks page (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/quickbooks-inventory/):
  "Your invoices are reflected in QuickBooks Online the moment you create them in Zoho Inventory." (Do not
  name QuickBooks as THEIR integration on the page: QuickBooks is one of OUR delivery formats; the table
  row says "an accounting sync".)
- ⇒ Zoho Inventory is the stock system of record. VOS is not; never imply VOS sees, counts or plans stock better.

## ⚠⚠ Customer portal: kitchens can log in, but CANNOT order there (the page's angle)
- Help, US (https://www.zoho.com/inventory/help/customer-portal/) and AU
  (https://www.zoho.com/au/inventory/help/customer-portal/): **"Your customers cannot add items and place
  orders directly from the customer portal."** Customers use it to **"view their sales orders, pay for
  invoices, check the status of their shipments, update their information, and much more."**
  "Customer portal is available only for certain plans of Zoho Inventory."
- Functions page (https://www.zoho.com/inventory/help/customer-portal/functions.html): sales orders viewable
  only if "enable[d] in customer portal preferences"; "If the invoice is unpaid, they can choose to pay for it
  instantly by clicking Pay Now."; statements; comments; PDFs.
- Pricing page lists "Customer portal" in the Standard plan.
⇒ The kitchen can see the order and pay for it in Zoho's portal; it cannot place one there. That is the
page's angle. Wording must stay scoped to the PORTAL (Zoho Commerce does take orders, below).

## ⚠ Customer ordering online: Zoho's answer is a separate product, Zoho Commerce
- Order management page: **"Sell online without another system. Build your online store with Zoho Commerce
  and manage its orders in Zoho Inventory."**
- Zoho Commerce KB (https://www.zoho.com/commerce/kb/general/does-it-support-b2b-and-b2c/): "Yes, both B2B and
  B2C selling takes place in the same Zoho Commerce store." **"B2C is available by default, while B2B tools
  like price lists and credit limits require the Premium plan or higher."** "You can also set up a separate
  B2B store with its own domain and branding".
- Zoho community threads ask for a B2B portal with per-customer pricing that writes sales orders into
  Inventory (help.zoho.com "B2B customer portal in zoho commerce"); third-party B2B portals sell connectors
  for exactly this. Not used on the page beyond "a Zoho Commerce store".
⇒ Never write "Zoho has no online ordering". Honest: online ordering for customers runs through a Zoho
Commerce store (typed, browsed), a separate Zoho product; B2B price lists sit on its higher plans.

## ⚠ Staff order entry and mobile
- Sales orders (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/sales-order-management/): **"You'll be able to create sales
  orders for your offline sales, and all your online sales orders will be automatically fetched into the
  system."** ⇒ phone/offline orders are created by staff.
- Mobile (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/order-management/): **"Raise and manage sales orders from anywhere
  using the Zoho Inventory mobile app on iOS or Android."**; barcode scanning to pick and verify. Mobile apps
  page (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/mobile-apps/): items, stock adjustments, picklists, returns: a team
  app ("Generate picklists for your warehouse staff"). Customers only receive notifications and payment links.
- Sales order import (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/kb/sales-order/so-import-export.html): **CSV or TSV
  only**, 1 MB limit, field mapping. Our emailed files are PDF/Excel/Word, so do NOT claim our file imports;
  the page says the emailed file is entered by the team.
- API: public REST API (https://www.zoho.com/inventory/api/v1/). EDI through partners (SPS Commerce, Crossfire,
  Infocon; Crossfire page: "saves you time from manually typing in orders and invoices from your trading
  partners"). Do not name the EDI partners on the page.

## ⚠ AI (Zia) and automation — staff-side; no voice ordering found
- AI page (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/features/ai-in-inventory/): "Use AI to ask questions in plain
  English"; **Ask Zia**: "Look up stock levels, check order statuses, or pull up customer records without
  navigating through modules"; Zia invoice insights; sentiment analysis; writing assistant; Blueprint

  "Create, approve, and track sales orders ... through AI agents".
- No speech input or customer voice ordering on that page or anywhere found on zoho.com/inventory. Zia voice
  exists in Zoho CRM (community pages), not described for Inventory.
- Autoscan (https://www.zoho.com/us/inventory/help/documents/documents.html): reads emailed/uploaded documents
  ("date, amount, merchant name") for transactions such as bills; no page says it creates sales orders.
  Pricing has an "Advanced autoscans" add-on. Not used on the page.
⇒ Zoho's AI works for the distributor's team. S8 reserves "their AI serves your staff" for sage-x3 and
prophet-21, so the page mentions Zia only inside the voice-tools FAQ and the sourcing FAQ.

## ⚠⚠ VOICE: none native, but third-party connectors already turn speech into Zoho Inventory sales orders
- Integrately (https://integrately.com/integrations/retell-ai/zoho-inventory): action **"Create sales order in
  Zoho Inventory"**, with "Retell AI lets you build, deploy, and monitor intelligent voice agents for calls".
- Zapier template (https://zapier.com/apps/amazon-alexa/integrations/zoho-inventory/1327619/...): **"Create Zoho
  Inventory sales orders when new Amazon Alexa trigger phrases are spoken"**.
- Others list Zoho Inventory as a voice-agent target (famulor.io, latenode "vapi", viasocket).
⇒ Never "nobody offers voice ordering for Zoho Inventory". Defensible difference, without naming them:
these are connections you assemble in an automation service, and with a phone voice agent the caller never
sees the lines; in our app the chef sees and fixes each line from their own guide before sending.

## Angle for the VOS page
**Zoho's portal already has your kitchens logged in, and it is the one place they cannot order.** Zoho
Inventory gives each customer a portal to view sales orders, pay invoices and track shipments, but
"customers cannot add items and place orders directly from the customer portal". So the kitchen that just
paid online still calls, texts or leaves a voicemail, and your team creates the sales order ("create sales
orders for your offline sales"). Zoho's own route to customer ordering is a separate Zoho Commerce store,
typed and browsed, with B2B price lists on its higher plans. VoiceOrder Solutions adds the missing step: the
kitchen says the order in an app, checks each line from its own guide (built from your Zoho price lists), and
sends it. Zoho keeps stock, batches, price lists, invoices and the portal.
- Distinct from NetSuite (licensed web-store module), Odoo (Order Again refills), and the ERP pages'
  replacement-project argument (kept to the cost FAQ only).

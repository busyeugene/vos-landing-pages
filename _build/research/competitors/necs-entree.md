# NECS entrée (necs.com) — competitor research for `/necs-entree-alternative`

**Verified live 2026-10-10.** No prior entry existed in the Writing repo's competitors file.

⚠ **Spell it "Entree" without the accent on our page.** Their site writes "entrée". `claims.js`
bans both spellings under named-integrations, and the compare-page exemption keys on the single
registry value `Entree`, which will not mask the accented form. Writing "Entree" also matches the
Airtable keyword (`necs entree alternative`).

## Positioning (verbatim)
- Heading: **"NECS ERP Software for Food Distributors"**
- Subhead: **"entrée V4 SQL: THE LATEST GENERATION IN FOOD DISTRIBUTION SOFTWARE"**; the current
  generation is **entrée V5**.
- **"NECS began in 1987"** and has served food distribution **"since 1987"**. Roughly four decades.
- Described as **"Windows based, food distribution software"**. Cloud is an option, not the
  default: "Run entrée in the Cloud", "Your Data, Your Way."

## This is a full ERP, and the module list is the point
entrée.AP, entrée.PAY, entrée.DOC, entrée.EDI, entrée.GL, entrée.MW, entrée.QB, entrée.WMS,
plus an EXPRESS API and a WMS API, Cloud Hosting, a System Optimizer and Food Distribution
University for training. Features named include a FAST Report Engine, cycle counts, dashboards
and a scheduling utility.

## ⚠ They ALREADY have customer-facing ordering. Do not claim otherwise.
| Module | Their words |
|---|---|
| entrée.EXPRESS | "Mobile Order Entry" — "Fast order entry on your customer's mobile device" |
| entrée.NET | "Web Order Entry, Reporting & more" — customers place their own orders online |
| entrée.EOP | "Electronic Order Pad - DSR Order Management on a tablet" |
| entrée.POD | "Proof-Of-Delivery" app |
| Website Catalog / WordPress Websites | customer-facing sites for distributors |

This kills the lazy version of this page. The distinction is **not** that they have no customer
ordering. It is that their order entry is **typed or tapped**, on web and mobile, as modules of
the ERP. None of it is voice, and all of it lives inside the system of record.

## Not published
- **No prices.** entrée.PAY is "Included FREE with entrée V4 and later"; a testimonial claims
  "the most affordable price" but no figure appears anywhere.
- **No implementation or onboarding process is described.** Do not invent a timeline for them, and
  do not imply adding one of their modules is an ERP migration: we do not know that.
- Support is "Emergency 24 Hour Support" plus remote support. Training is on-demand e-learning.

⚠ Their navigation is duplicated in two inconsistent versions (entrée.PAY appears as both
"Accounts Payable" and "Credit Card & ACH Processing"). That is a site fault, not a product fact,
and it stays out of our copy.

## The angle for this page (must differ from all six before it)
Entree is the **system of record**, and for most people searching this keyword the honest answer
is that they should not replace it. Four decades of accounting, GL, AP, EDI and warehouse
functions are not something you swap out to fix how orders arrive. So this page argues **against
the premise of its own keyword**, which no other page in the set does:

> If you are looking for an Entree alternative, you may not need one. You may need one thing to
> change, and the rest to stay exactly where it is.

The two real, checkable distinctions:
1. **Typed or tapped, versus spoken.** Their customer ordering is web and mobile order entry. VOS
   is voice, with the buyer approving the lines.
2. **Inside the ERP, versus beside it.** Their ordering is a module of the system of record, tied
   to its version and its upgrade path. VOS sits outside and hands the finished order in.

**Concede generously, because it is all true:** Entree runs the business. It carries accounting,
general ledger, payables, EDI, warehouse management, proof of delivery and reporting. VOS has none
of that and never will. Give Entree the first rows of the table.

**Be fair about the age.** 1987 is depth of domain knowledge, not a flaw, and a distributor running
it for twenty years has a working system. Never frame longevity as decay.

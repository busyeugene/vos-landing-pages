**Verified live 2026-10-10.** Supersedes the 2026-07-06 entry in the Writing repo.

**Slug ruling:** the primary keyword is `cut+dry alternative` and a `+` cannot go in a URL. The
client's own live blog post sits at `/blog/cut-dry-alternatives`, so the house precedent is
`cut-dry`. This page is `/cut-dry-alternative`. Decided on that evidence, not a guess.

## Pricing — PARITY, do not attack
There IS a `/distributors/pricing/` page and **it publishes no price, tier or fee.** It is a
marketing page with customer stats and a "Book a Demo" button. So both products are quote-based
and the pricing-parity rule applies.

## Implementation
Only one data point, and it belongs to a named customer: **Y. Hata & Co., "90 days from contract
to launch."** There is no general timeline. Attribute it to that customer, or do not use it.

## The angle for this page (must differ from Pepper, Choco, Burnt and GrubMarket)
Cut+Dry is a **three-sided network**, and its own site says so. The honest, checkable contrast is
**who else is in the room when your customer places an order**:
- Manufacturers pay to promote SKUs to operators "at the exact moment they're building an order".
  That is their own description of the product, from their own manufacturer page.
- Restaurants are offered **cashback from participating distributors and manufacturers**.
- So the ordering moment on that platform carries other parties' commercial interests.
- With VOS the account speaks from **your** guide at **your** prices. Nobody is promoting into
  that moment, and there is no network to be ranked inside.

**Concede plainly:** Cut+Dry does things VOS does not, and the page should say so: digital
payments and invoicing, a sales control centre, product-data syndication, and an ecommerce
storefront with search and discovery. VOS has none of that.

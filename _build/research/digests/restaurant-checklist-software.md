# Digest: restaurant checklist software (operator page)

## 1. SERP makeup
Every result is a restaurant task / checklist / compliance tool: product pages (Opsi task lists, Xenia, Intouch Insight),
vendor listicles dressed as landing pages (Operandio x2, Mitti), and articles (Connecteam listicle, Restaurant365 how-to,
Blanket, Railwaymen). #1 (Jolt LP) now redirects to a SmartSense IoT temperature page, so it was dropped; one extra source
fetched (Blanket, extra-1.md). Buyer = restaurant operators, mostly multi-unit / QSR / franchise. Same person VOS serves on
this page, but a different job: shift tasks, food safety, audits. Ordering shows up only as a line item ("Scheduling,
ordering, receiving, inventory", restaurant365.com; "inventories, orders, and deliveries", connecteam.com). No ranking
tool places the order. Searcher is close to VOS's user, far from VOS's feature set: honesty about scope is essential.

## 5. Where our page is weaker
- Hero: eyebrow repeats the H1 word for word; lead never uses the paper-to-digital framing the whole SERP leads with.
- Stats: "Same" is not a number.
- Problem: lead promises "what that inconsistency costs" but doesn't name memory as the cause; card 3 repeats "skip the parts".
- Feature row 1: opens with a claim about every kitchen; never states the gap the SERP leaves (checklists say "do the order", nothing places it).
- Feature cards: H2 "All the tools your team needs" oversells; lead "No extra apps" contradicts FAQ 1 ("pair VOS with an ops app").
- Accountability is table stakes in the SERP and never named.
- Comparison lead is apologetic and reuses FAQ 1's "we would rather"; it doesn't say what VOS does that task apps don't.
- FAQ 7 and 8 use sitewide question wording, not how this SERP asks.
- UK words on a US page: "on holiday", "cleaning rotas".

## 6. Where VOS genuinely differs
- It places the order. Task apps can remind someone to order; VOS is where the order is built, checked and sent. Lead the comparison with this.
- No checklist to build: the distributor builds and updates the order guide (SERP names checklist setup as upfront effort).
- Voice while walking the cooler, then review before sending. Say it plainly, no "AI" framing.
- Same-day first order through the distributor (SERP talks in weeks of rollout); do not name competitors.
- Scope honesty is the edge: keep FAQ 1 and the ✗ rows, and tell operators to keep their task app for everything else.

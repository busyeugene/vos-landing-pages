# Digest: order-entry-software (operator page)

## 1. SERP makeup
Nine usable pages (Esker scrape failed). Five product pages: IntelliChief, Workist, Digit, LaceUp, Sage; four articles: monday.com, MRPeasy,
DCKAP, WizCommerce listicles. Every one is **seller-side**: software that captures a customer's incoming order (email, PDF, fax, rep
tablet) and keys it into the seller's ERP or accounting system. Industries: manufacturing, B2B wholesale/distribution, some ecommerce
(Sage, MRPeasy, monday). Zero pages speak to a restaurant, and zero speak to the buyer placing the order. **Distance from this page's
audience: far.** The searcher is a distributor/manufacturer back office, i.e. VOS's real buyer, which the order-taking page serves.
This page stays operator-facing; only framings that are true from the kitchen's seat are borrowed.

## 6. Where VOS genuinely differs
- Built for the person placing the order, not the office keying it in. Every SERP tool cleans up an order after it arrives; VOS gets it
  right at the source (own guide, checked on screen, then sent). Lean on this in the comparison heading and lead.
- Voice from the walk-in, 24/7, iOS + Android. No SERP page offers the buyer anything to use.
- Per-account order guide at the distributor's prices and item numbers, kept current by the distributor.
- Nothing to replace on either side: sent as email file, EDI, API, QuickBooks or direct integration.
- Set up by the distributor; most restaurants order the same day (vs "2 days" to "months" in the SERP).

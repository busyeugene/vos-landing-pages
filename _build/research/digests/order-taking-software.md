# Digest: order-taking-software (distributor page)

## 1. SERP makeup
Google reads "order taking software" as *order management software (OMS)*. 8 scraped: 1 B2B landing page (simplydepo.com,
wholesale/DSD reps + buyer portal), 2 vendor pages written as guides (sage.com accounting/ERP, zoho.com SMB inventory),
1 enterprise guide (microsoft.com Dynamics 365), 4 listicles/articles (theretailexec, monday.com, croclub, zite). Industries:
mostly ecommerce/retail multichannel and ERP/accounting; one B2B wholesale page; zero restaurant or food-distribution pages.
The searcher is only partly VOS's buyer: the closest match is SimplyDepo's "small-to-mid-market wholesale distributors, DSD".
Direct competitors (Pepper, Choco, Cut+Dry, Burnt) do not rank but sell the same job to food distributors.

## 2. How competitors frame the tool
1. **Every order captured in one place** (6/8 + choco, burnt): "Capture every order, in person or online" (simplydepo), "captures every order, everywhere" (choco)
2. **End manual order entry and its errors** (7/8 + pepper, choco): "saying goodbye to manual data entry errors" (sage), "End manual order entry" (choco)
3. **Only sell what's in stock** (7/8): "orders are right the first time" (simplydepo), "no more awkward 'we're out of that' emails" (theretailexec)
4. **Plugs into your ERP / accounting** (8/8 + pepper, choco): "Native QuickBooks Online sync" (simplydepo), "feeds it straight into your ERP" (choco)
5. **Reps sell instead of taking orders** (simplydepo + pepper, choco, cut+dry): "more time selling, less time taking orders" (pepper quote)
6. **24/7 ordering, business never sleeps** (1/8 + choco, cut+dry): "Buyers reorder 24/7 at their own pricing" (simplydepo)
7. **Customer-specific pricing / catalog** (simplydepo + cut+dry, laceup): "Every customer opens with its own price list" (simplydepo)
8. **Fast go-live** (simplydepo, monday FAQ + pepper, cut+dry, burnt): "most teams are live in days" (simplydepo); others cite 35 to 90 days

## 3. Vocabulary searchers expect
- ✅ "take orders" / "capture" orders (verified: after-hours orders are captured): used, "capture" missing
- ✅ "manual order entry" / "keyed in by hand": weak, say it plainly once
- ✅ "ERP" and "accounting software" as places orders go (KB: integrates with ERPs): missing, page only says "distribution platform"
- ✅ push / text confirmation (KB): missing
- ✅ "your SKUs / product codes, customer-specific prices": present, can sharpen into "nothing to match or look up"
- ✅ "order desk" (who takes phone orders at a distributor): missing
- ☑ "24/7", "real time", "stockouts", "errors", "QuickBooks", "EDI", "order history", "sales reps", "iOS and Android": used well
- ❌ "multichannel / omnichannel" (VOS is one channel, it does not ingest email/voicemail/texts)
- ❌ "self-serve portal / storefront / ecommerce" (web ordering unconfirmed)
- ❌ "offline order capture", "sales order lifecycle", "order status placed to delivered" (unconfirmed; tracking is a sibling page)
- ❌ "pick, pack, ship, labels, routing, returns, invoicing" (WMS/OMS, not VOS)
- ❌ "AI", "larger average order size", "scale without headcount" (not in KB)

## 4. FAQ and objection themes
Setup time (monday, simplydepo; competitors 35 to 90 days) → answered. Integrations with ERP/accounting/CRM (croclub, simplydepo,
pepper, choco) → answered as "formats", but never says ERP. Mobile/offline (simplydepo) → mobile answered. Pricing (simplydepo,
theretailexec, croclub) → answered, quote-based. Who is it for / small business (simplydepo, zite) → answered. OMS vs CRM, "is this
a full OMS?" (simplydepo, croclub, zite) → NOT answered. Will customers actually use it (cut+dry quote about customers who
refused online ordering) → NOT answered. Do I replace my system → answered.

## 5. Where our page is weaker
- Hero lead opens with a definition, then features; competitors open with the outcome (orders in, no hand entry).
- Features H2 "fits how your team works" is vague; says nothing about the result (written, clean orders).
- 24/7 row: "Restaurants order when they count stock" edges toward restaurant counts; two bullets both say weekends; "nothing to install or maintain" unverified.
- Order guide row claims accounts "will actually use" it "week after week" (unverified adoption).
- Cards: "Direct Platform Integration" never says ERP; "Confirmed Before Sending" omits push/text; "Error Reduction" is a label, not an outcome.
- Comparison lead talks about restaurants buying, not the distributor; two "Generic Software ✗" cells are wrong (24/7 portals and real-time stock are standard in generic OMS per 7/8 SERP pages).
- FAQ: no honest "VOS is not a full order management system" line, though the SERP is all OMS; no adoption question.

## 6. Where VOS genuinely differs
- Clean at the source: pepper/choco/burnt read messy voicemails, texts and emails with AI and guess; VOS customers speak into their own
  order guide and confirm before sending. Lean on "confirmed by the customer, in your SKUs, nothing to decode".
- Voice, hands-free, from the walk-in: storefronts and portals (cut+dry, simplydepo) need typing.
- 24 to 48 hours live, first orders same day, no IT project, vs 35 to 90 day go-lives and "70+ ERP" projects.
- Additive: orders land as email PDF/Excel/Word, EDI, API, QuickBooks. A small shop without an ERP still gets clean orders.
- Built for small to mid-size food distributors and DSD, not generic ecommerce OMS.

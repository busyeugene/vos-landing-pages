# Digest: inventory automation software (distributor page)

## 1. SERP makeup
8 scraped (7 unique; safetyculture.com and mitti.com are the same listicle). Vendor pages: Zoho Inventory, Sortly, inFlow,
Sage (all general SMB inventory management). Listicles: Mitti "Top 10 inventory control", Novo "best inventory software for
small business", Procurement Magazine top 10. Google reads the query as "inventory management software with automation".
Industries: ecommerce/multichannel (Shopify, Amazon), retail, light manufacturing, warehouses, construction/medical supplies.
Distribution appears only as a side mention (Acctivate "SMB distribution inventory", Unleashed and Cin7 "wholesalers"). No food
distribution, no restaurant pages. The searcher is a small-business owner replacing spreadsheets and hand counts. Far from
VOS's buyer; the overlap is "stop entering things by hand" and "stock that updates as orders happen".
Direct competitors (Choco, Pepper, Burnt, Cut+Dry, LaceUp) sell the distributor version: end order typing, 24/7 capture, reps sell.

## 3. Vocabulary searchers expect
- ✅ "real time" / "real-time stock levels": VOS live site says real-time inventory tied to orders. Not used on our page.
- ✅ "stock levels": used once (bullet). Weak in body copy.
- ✅ "manual entry" / "by hand" / "hand-keyed": VOS live site uses "manual re-entry", "hand-keyed". Page says "re-keyed" (jargon-ish).
- ✅ "order errors" / "mistakes": only "wrong quantity" on page.
- ✅ "reps sell instead of taking orders": live site "reps stop being order takers". Page frames reps as "chasing stock counts".
- ✅ "order history": ☑ card and admin screenshot. Fine.
- ☑ "spreadsheets", "stockouts", "after hours", "QuickBooks / EDI / API", "no hardware", "iPhone and Android": used well.
- ☑ "integrations / works with your ERP": FAQ 1, but asked as "switch systems" rather than "work with our ERP".
- ❌ "reorder points", "automatic reordering", "low-stock alerts", "purchase orders": not VOS. Say so in existing FAQ only.
- ❌ "barcode scanning", "bin locations", "label printing": not VOS. Already in the WMS FAQ + comparison row.
- ❌ "multichannel sync", "Shopify/Amazon", "demand forecasting", "overstocking": not VOS. Do not adopt.
- ❌ "sales orders" (implies ERP sales-order creation, unverified): avoid.

- Do I need barcode scanning / scanners? (Novo FAQ, inFlow hardware). Ours: WMS FAQ + hardware FAQ, but not asked that way.
- Works with my accounting/ERP? (Novo, Sage). Ours: FAQ 1 ("switch systems"). Answered.
- Pricing / how much to spend (Novo FAQ, Zoho, Mitti tables). Ours: only "Pricing on request" in final CTA. Not in FAQ.
- Setup time (Novo "within a day", Cin7 "a week"). Ours: FAQ 8. Answered.
- Small business fit (inFlow, Novo). Ours: FAQ 7. Answered. Mobile (Sortly, Zoho): FAQ 5. Answered.

## 5. Where our page is weaker
- Hero: H1 "Handles the Busywork" is vague; no concrete task named. "busywork" appears 7 times across the page.
- Stats: "Auto" is not a number. A verified number (24/7) is sitting in the trust row instead.
- Feature 1: no "real time" or "stock levels", the two terms every SERP page treats as table stakes.
- Feature 1 reps line is off-message; VOS's own framing is reps selling instead of taking orders.
- FAQ 2 ends "People still make the calls", which reads as phone calls on a page about ending phone calls.
- FAQ 3 and 4 do not use the words searchers use (reorder automatically, barcode scanning).
- Comparison lead talks only about warehouse suites; the SERP alternative is a general inventory app with scanners.
- FAQ 8 repeats "24 to 48 hours" already in the table (within-page repeat).

## 6. Where VOS genuinely differs
- Automation starts at the order, not the stockroom: orders captured 24/7 in an app (voice), arrive in the distributor's
  format, and stock and catalog update from that order activity. No scanners, no counts, no reorder rules.
- Built for small to mid-size food distributors; live in 24 to 48 hours; nothing to replace (sits beside ERP/accounting).
- Per-account order guides with the distributor's SKUs and prices; the customer confirms every order (control stays human).
- Lean on: "the typing and stock updates happen for you; you and your customers still decide". Be explicit that auto-reorder
  and barcodes are not part of it, so the searcher from the general SERP self-qualifies.

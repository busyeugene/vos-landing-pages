# Digest: restaurant-purchasing-software (operator page)

## 1. SERP makeup
8 scraped of 10: 5 product landing pages (MarketMan, Supy, ChefMod, Gofrugal, CheddrSuite), 1 homepage (Craftable),
1 listicle (tabcommerce "9 best restaurant procurement software"), 1 product/procurement page (Apicbase). All restaurant
(and hotel) back-office suites: purchasing + inventory + invoices/AP + recipe costing, sold to multi-unit groups with a
buyer or finance team. The searcher IS a restaurant operator, so very close to VOS's page audience, but they expect a
suite. VOS covers only the ordering step, and reaches them through their distributor. Nobody in the SERP sells voice.

## 2. How competitors frame the tool
1. All vendors / suppliers in one place (8/8): "All Your Vendors, One App" (marketman), "Order from all suppliers at once" (apicbase), "Centralized supplier management" (chefmod)
2. Kill the calls, texts and emails (5/8): "No more emails, texts, calls, or faxing" (marketman), "no calls, e-mail or texts are required" (apicbase)
3. Purchase orders sent to each supplier (8/8): "submit purchase orders, check statuses" (marketman), "customise POs" (supy)
4. Spend and food-cost control, price tracking/comparison (8/8): "tracks ingredient prices across all of your vendors" (marketman), "Prevent rogue spending" (apicbase)
5. Automated / suggested orders from pars or forecasts (6/8): "AI builds purchase orders. You hit send." (supy), "Par stock ordering" (apicbase)
6. Invoice processing, receiving, 3-way match (7/8): "AI-powered Receiving" (marketman), "Invoice reconciliation" (apicbase)
7. Multi-site control and who-ordered-what transparency (6/8): "managers place orders transparently" (marketman), "HQ can monitor orders" (apicbase)
8. Mobile, from anywhere / the walk-in (5/8): "Effortless ordering, anytime, anywhere" (chefmod), "on a phone in the walk-in" (tabcommerce)

## 3. Vocabulary searchers expect
- ✅ vendors (SERP says "vendors" as often as "suppliers"; our page never did) -> added once
- ✅ calls, texts, emails, portals (our list had no email) -> added
- ✅ purchase order (as the job an order does, not a PO module) -> in feature 2 body, FAQ reworded
- ✅ shorts / wrong item on delivery, check delivery vs order (manual check against the record) -> feature 2
- ✅ item codes / SKUs tied to the order guide (verified) -> feature 4
- ☑ order guide, one app, multi-location, mobile app, 24/7, order history, negotiated prices, time saved (20-30 min)
- ☑ approvals / permissions: correctly absent
- ❌ price comparison / price tracking, food cost, rogue spend, budgets
- ❌ invoice scanning, AP automation, receiving, 3-way match
- ❌ par levels, suggested POs, demand forecasting, AI ordering (par levels unconfirmed for operators)
- ❌ recipe costing, POS integration, central kitchen / transfers

## 4. FAQ and objection themes
- Multiple suppliers in one place (MarketMan, Apicbase): answered (Q2).
- Does it create POs (CheddrSuite, MarketMan): answered (Q3), reworded to how it is asked.
- Multi-location (Gofrugal, Apicbase): answered (Q5, primary keyword).
- Compare prices / cost control (Gofrugal, CheddrSuite): answered "no" (Q4); widened to invoices and recipe costs.
- POS / accounting integrations (4 FAQs): not a VOS feature; covered by the widened Q4 "what VOS is not" answer.
- Setup / implementation weight (tabcommerce, Supy quote): answered (Q8).
- What does it replace (Craftable): partly in Q1; comparison table lead now says it.
- Par / automated reorder, receiving: not answered on purpose (unverified / not VOS).

## 5. Where our page was weaker
- H1 "Without the Paperwork": paperwork reads as invoices/AP, which VOS does not do. SERP's top promise is suppliers in one app.
- Hero lead opened with a definition, not the outcome.
- Problem list skipped email; the same "phone line, two portals, text thread" list sat in the card, feature row 1 and FAQ 2 (trigram repeats).
- Feature 2 H3 feature-first ("with a record"); SERP pain is proving what was ordered when a case is short.
- Feature 4 did not say guides are tied to the supplier's own item codes (verified, concrete).
- Comparison framed rivals as "corporate procurement" suites needing "months". The real SERP rivals are restaurant
  back-office suites; some cells were wrong (suites do have order guides and 24/7 ordering).
- FAQ 4 only denied price comparison; the SERP's invoice and recipe-costing expectations went unanswered.

## 6. Where VOS genuinely differs
- Nothing to buy or run: the distributor sets up the account and loads the guide; first order usually the same day.
- Voice ordering from the walk-in, at any hour. No suite in the SERP offers it.
- Guides at the prices already set for the account; keeps the rep and the deal (vs GPO or price-shopping pitches).
- Does one job (the order) and says so, so an operator can keep any back-office tool they already use.
Lean on: "one app, many suppliers", "proof of what you ordered", "set up by your distributor", and an honest
"not a back-office suite" comparison.

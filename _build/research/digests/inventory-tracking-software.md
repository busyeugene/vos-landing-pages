inFlow home, Zoho "free inventory" landing, Square POS inventory feature) and 4 listicles (Forbes, Mitti/SafetyCulture,

slot (inFlow "Best for Wholesale and B2B", Unleashed, Acctivate). No food distributor or restaurant-supply page ranks;
restaurants appear only through Square POS. Searcher = an owner who counts their own shelves and wants off
spreadsheets. Distance from VOS's buyer: far. Overlap is narrow: small/mid-size, off spreadsheets, know what's on hand,
fewer stockouts. Direct competitors (Pepper, Choco, Cut+Dry) don't sell inventory tracking; Burnt only mentions live
inventory checks inside order intake.

## 6. Where VOS genuinely differs
- Generic apps learn about stock from staff scanning or POS sales. VOS learns from the customer's order itself, placed
  in the app, confirmed and timestamped. Nobody types in a phone order to update stock.
- Customers order 24/7 by voice, so after-hours orders are counted before the team opens.
- The catalog updates as stock changes; each account orders from its own guide (your prices, your SKUs).
- Built for small to mid-size food distributors; live in 24 to 48 hours; no rip-and-replace; orders arrive as
  email/PDF/Excel/Word, EDI, API or QuickBooks.
- Lean on it: "tracking that runs on orders, not scanners"; comparison lead contrasts typing/scanning with the order
  updating stock; answer the spreadsheet question in the FAQ.

# Digest: inventory visibility software (distributor page)

## 1. SERP makeup
8 scraped: 2 landing pages (zebra.com Workcloud Inventory Visibility = retail store cycle counts, shrink, returns;
scnsoft.com = custom supply chain visibility builds, $200k+) and 6 articles (r4.ai enterprise "DecisionOps", netsuite.com
ERP guide, shipbob.com ecommerce 3PL, learn.microsoft.com Dynamics 365 Inventory Visibility Add-in docs, netstock.com SMB
planning blog, xorosoft.com ERP guide for Shopify/Amazon/wholesale). Industries: enterprise supply chain, omnichannel
retail/ecommerce, 3PL, manufacturing. Wholesale distribution shows up only as a subsection (xorosoft, netsuite); food only
as lot/expiry tracking. No food-distributor ordering tool ranks. Intent is informational and enterprise, far from VOS's
buyer. The overlap is narrow but real: a distributor whose reps and customers need to see what is available right now.

## 2. How competitors frame the tool
1. **Everyone works from one number** (6/8): "one accurate view of where stock sits" (r4.ai); "single source of truth" (netstock.com); "when every system shows a different number" (xorosoft.com).
2. **Real-time, not stale** (8/8): "decisions made on stale data" (netsuite.com); "up-to-the-minute inventory counts" (zebra.com); "avoid making decisions from outdated numbers" (xorosoft.com).
3. **Fewer stockouts / overselling / backorders** (6/8): "Avoid overselling" (learn.microsoft.com); "no need for back orders" (netstock.com).
4. **The customer promise: what sales can commit to** (4/8): "Sales teams need to know what they can promise" (xorosoft.com); "confidently answer availability questions" (netsuite.com); "available-to-promise" (learn.microsoft.com).
5. **Sits on top of ERP/WMS, no rip and replace** (5/8): "without rip and replace" (r4.ai); "does not replace warehouse management" (r4.ai); connects ERP, POS, WMS by API (learn.microsoft.com).
6. **Spreadsheets and manual counts break** (4/8): "spreadsheets become slow, fragile" (xorosoft.com); "can't keep up" (netstock.com).
7. **Visibility is not the same as tracking** (3/8): "Tracking answers, 'What happened?'" (xorosoft.com); "goes beyond basic stock counting" (netsuite.com).
8. **Multi-location, in-transit, RFID/barcode, cycle counts** (7/8): the bulk of the category's feature lists. Not VOS.

## 5. Where our page is weaker
- **H1** "for Your Team and Customers" is vague. SERP's one outcome is "everyone sees the same number". Say that.
- **Hero lead** lacks "real time" and does not say stock updates because of orders. Easy fix.
- **Problem H2** "different answer to the same question" is abstract; name the pain: different stock numbers.
- **Feature 1 bullets**: "version confusion" is jargon; bullet 4 repeats the body ("not a copy each").
- **Feature 2 bullet 3** repeats the auto catalog update fact already in feature 3 and FAQ 7.
- **Feature 4 bullet** "No retraining for your staff" is not in the KB. Swap for the verified "adds to what your platform does".
- **FAQ**: no "what is" definition; Q1 and setup FAQ wording is shared by 10+ sibling pages; Tier C answer lists WMS
  terms but not the SERP's big ❌ themes (RFID, cycle counts, in-transit).
- **Within-page repeat**: "24 to 48 hours" twice (hero trust + FAQ).

## 6. Where VOS genuinely differs
- The category sells an internal view (dashboards for planners). VOS pushes availability **out to the customer's order
  guide** while they order. That is the page's strongest angle: lean on "customers see what is out before they order".
- Built for small to mid-size food distributors, live in 24 to 48 hours. SERP talks months of implementation and $200k builds.
- Visibility is **tied to order activity**, not to scanners, RFID or counting hardware. Say "every order moves the number".
- Additive to the ERP, API-ready: same "no rip and replace" promise enterprise tools make, without the ERP project.

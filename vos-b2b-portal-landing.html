Ordering By Voice

Talking beats hunting through a catalog.

Most B2B portal software asks a buyer to search, filter and click a path to 40 line items. In VOS your customer says them instead, reads the whole list back on screen, and sends it. That difference decides whether a B2B buyer portal gets used again next week.

  • ✓Talk down the list in that account's own guide
  • ✓Each line shown for review before anything is sent
  • ✓Interrupted mid-order? The app holds their place
  • ✓A push notice or a text says the order arrived

Orders In Your Format

Orders turn up in a shape your office can use.

A B2B customer portal is only worth having if what comes out of it fits the way your office works. We agree the delivery route with you up front, and orders take it from then on: a file in your inbox, a QuickBooks entry, or a feed wired straight into the system you run.

  • ✓You pick the format once, during onboarding
  • ✓With EDI, QuickBooks or the API, nobody keys the order again

  • ✓Your ERP, your pricing rules and your invoicing stay put

FAQ

Straight answers on portals and VOS

It does the job distributors buy a portal for: your customers serve themselves, from their own item list, at the prices you gave them, at any hour. It does it through an app they speak to rather than a website they browse, and it holds no storefront, shopping cart or payment handling. Your team's side, where guides and accounts are managed, runs on the web.
Ordering happens on a phone, in the app. The web side is where your own staff edit guides, open accounts and look up past orders. We would rather be plain about that than have you promise your accounts a desktop screen.
A webshop is built around browsing and checkout. A kitchen reordering 40 familiar items every week is not browsing. VOS starts from the guide you built for that account, so the buyer talks through a list they already know, checks it, and sends it. Nothing to search for.
A guide for every buyer, and a decision on how orders should reach you. That is the whole lift. Unlike B2B portal software you commission, there is nothing to design, host or patch. Most distributors are up and running in 24 to 48 hours, and most of their customers send something in that same day.
Yes. Orders can arrive as a PDF, Excel or Word attachment, through EDI, into QuickBooks, or over an API link to whatever platform your office runs on. On the EDI, QuickBooks and API routes nobody enters it a second time. Emailed files may still be keyed in, which is worth weighing when you choose.
No. We are not an agency and we do not take build work, custom front-ends, or bespoke integrations beyond the delivery formats listed above. VOS is a finished product you switch on. If what you need really is a bespoke portal, a development shop is the right call, not us.
No. Travel portals book flights, hotels and cars out of global distribution systems, and VOS does none of that. VOS serves food distributors who supply restaurants and grocery stores. If you arrived here after travel booking, this is the wrong product.
Nothing breaks. Your team writes those up the way it does today, and those calls carry no VOS number or timestamp. A sensible start is the customers who order most often, then let the rest follow once they see it working.

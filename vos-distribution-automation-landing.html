Sound Familiar?

Six people touch an order before anything gets picked.

Nobody designed it this way. It accumulated. Three stretches of that journey still done entirely by hand:

☎️

"Listen, write, read back"

Someone takes the call, scribbles the list, repeats it, then fixes whatever got misheard. Twenty minutes per customer.

⌨️

"Then it gets typed in again"

The same lines go from a pad into a screen, matched to codes from memory, with one more chance to go wrong.

📣

"And somebody rings them back"

To confirm it, to query a quantity, or to say an item is out. Another call, another note, another gap.

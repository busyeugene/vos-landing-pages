Why Teams Compare

Why distributors weigh an alternative to Fresho

Looking up a Fresho competitor is seldom a complaint about the product. It is usually a question of scope or of sequence, and three versions of it come round reliably.

🧰

Most of it is already running here

Fresho reaches from the order through to the payment, and their pricing lets you take only the parts you want. The question is how much of your operation you want one vendor inside.

🔁

Keeping the habit keeps the habit

Their AI is built so a customer need not change a thing, which is a deliberate choice and a sound one. The flip side is that the voicemails and the texts carry on, and a machine reads every one of them while your team approves what it produces.

📏

The opening move is sized like a project

They publish around four weeks to get set up, plus two to four weeks training the AI before it goes live, on top of the one-off fee. That is clear and fair. It is still a bigger first move than some distributors want when the whole aim is to stop the phone ringing at four.

When VOS and Fresho work together

This is a sensible pairing rather than a standoff. VOS is not a Fresho replacement: it changes the way some accounts order, and everything after that order, the picking and the run and the invoice, stays exactly where it is. A Fresho alternative quite often means precisely this.

🧾

The back end keeps every job it has

Invoices, payments, run sheets and pick lists are not on offer here and are not touched. VOS hands the completed order over and leaves the rest of the day alone.

📬

Fewer messages left to be read

Accounts that move across stop leaving voicemails and texts. Anything you have reading those gets a shorter list, which is the simplest way to improve it.

🧷

Small enough to try properly

No modules to choose and nothing to decommission. Open a few of the accounts that cost you the most time and decide on the evidence.

Your team can't start picking until someone types it in.

Orders arrive by phone, text, email, and voicemail, in five different formats, from customers who all say things a little differently. Here's what that costs you every morning:

📞

"Half our orders are still voicemails"

Someone sits there each morning playing them back and typing them up. That's an hour gone before a single case moves, and it's an hour where mistakes creep in.

❓

"We picked the wrong size again"

The order said "a case of tomatoes" and nobody knew which pack. Now it's on the truck, it's wrong, and you're eating a credit plus a second trip.

🌙

"Customers order when we're closed"

Chefs place orders at 11pm after service. If your only intake is a phone line during business hours, those orders wait until morning and your cutoff gets tighter.

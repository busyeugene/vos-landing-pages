Low-Stock Alerts

Get a heads-up before you run out.

Set a par level for each item and VOS watches it for you. When stock drops below par, you get an alert, so you reorder before the shelf is empty, not after. This inventory tracking system catches shortfalls before they turn into an 86'd menu item.

Sound Familiar?

Orders arrive faster than anyone can count stock.

Plenty of food distributors still track inventory on clipboards, spreadsheets or gut instinct. The numbers lag behind, and nobody sees the gap until something goes wrong:

📝

"We promised a case we didn't have"

An order came in for an item that sold out yesterday. Now your rep has to call about a substitution, and the account takes a hit.

📦

"Our stock sheet is always a day behind"

Orders arrive by phone, email and voicemail. The spreadsheet only catches up once someone keys them in, and by then the numbers are stale.

🕐

"My reps chase stock counts all day"

Before they confirm an order, reps walk to the back or call the office to ask what's left. That's time they aren't spending on their accounts.

Catalog Updates

Your catalog changes when your stock does.

When a stock level shifts, VOS updates the catalog on its own. Each customer orders from a guide you control, and the catalog behind it stays in sync with your stock. That means fewer sold-out surprises and fewer awkward calls to your customers.

  • ✓ Catalog updates automatically as stock levels change
  • ✓ You manage the catalog each order guide draws from
  • ✓ Fewer last-minute substitutions on delivery day
  • ✓ No one retypes catalog changes by hand

Sound Familiar?

The order came in. So why is your phone still ringing?

When orders show up by phone, voicemail and text, nobody has one record of what came in or when. So customers phone to check. Your team digs for the answer. Here's what that costs you:

🚚

"Did you get my order?"

A chef left a voicemail at 10pm. At 7am they call again to make sure it landed, and your rep has to find it before anyone can say yes.

📉

"We sent that before cutoff"

The account says they ordered in time. Their order is buried in a voicemail or text, so proving the time takes digging. You squeeze it onto the truck or start your morning with an unhappy customer.

🔍

"That's the wrong item"

They say you sent the wrong thing. If the order was called in, it's their word against your rep's notes, so you end up issuing a credit.

Orders ship short when nobody sees low stock in time.

Most shortages aren't about a product you couldn't buy. They happen because the stock numbers lagged behind demand, or the orders that should have warned you sat in voicemail until it was too late to reorder. Here's how it goes:

😬

"We found out it was gone when we went to pick it"

The Restock Call

Automated inventory replenishment software, minus the automatic reorder.

The automatic part is the order data. Each order from an account arrives digitized and timestamped, with no voicemail to type up, and your stock numbers change with it. The reorder decision stays with your buyer. VOS does not draft purchase orders to your suppliers, and it never sends an order by itself.

  • ✓Each order is tagged with a unique order number and a timestamp
  • ✓Order history shows what each account really buys
  • ✓Your buyer reorders in the tools already in place
  • ✓No purchase orders created on your behalf

Sound Familiar?

The walk-in runs low, and the reorder is still a phone call.

Most kitchens check stock on a clipboard, then read the list to a rep. Every handoff is a chance for the order to go wrong. Here's what it costs you:

🥩

"We ran out in the middle of service"

The order went in by voicemail and a quantity got missed. Now you're 86'ing a dish on a Saturday night and sending someone to the store at retail price.

📦

"We ordered six cases of what we already had"

Someone wrote the list at the desk from memory, not at the shelf. Now the extra cases sit in the back going bad, and that's cash you can't get back.

🗒️

"We saw it was low at close, then forgot"

The phone line was shut when you checked the walk-in at 11pm. By the morning rush the note was lost, and the order never went in.

Simple Setup

Set up by your distributor, ordering the same day

No IT team and no system to rip out. Here's how you go from setup to your first voice order.

1

Get your account from your distributor

They add your restaurant and upload its order guide. You get an app login tied to it. Not on VOS yet? Ask them, or contact us.

2

Sign in on your phone

Put VOS on your iPhone or Android and use that login. For most kitchens, the first order goes in that day.

3

Look, say it, send it

Check your shelves, open the app, and say what you need. Review the order, tap send, and you're back in the kitchen.

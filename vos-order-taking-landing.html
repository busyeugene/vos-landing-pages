Sound Familiar?

Taking orders by phone is costing your team hours.

Many food distributors still take orders the way they did 20 years ago: calls, voicemails, texts and emailed lists, all typed in by hand. Here's what that manual order entry costs you every week:

📞

"My reps spend all morning on order calls"

A customer phones in. A rep writes the order down, then types it into your system. Repeat that for every customer, and your reps and order desk end up doing data entry when they could be selling.

📋

"We shipped 15 cases. They wanted 5."

Phone orders get misheard. Voicemails get copied down wrong. The mistake only shows up on delivery day, and you pay for the return and the unhappy customer.

⏰

"A customer tried to order late Saturday, and nobody was in"

Your office closes at 5pm. Your customers run out on the weekend. They either wait, or they call a competitor who is still answering.

Simple Setup

No project, no cutover, no retraining

The reason this is quick is that nothing underneath it moves. Three steps and the front end behaves differently.

1

Tell us what a customer buys

Items, your prices, your codes. However you hold that today is almost certainly good enough.

2

Choose where orders should land

An inbox, QuickBooks, EDI or the API. Whichever your office handles best today.

3

We provision each account

For most distributors that is a day or two, then buyers download it. After go-live you change guides and prices yourself on the web; we only do the initial load.

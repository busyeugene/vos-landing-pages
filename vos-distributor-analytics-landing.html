Sound Familiar?

Nobody argues with the chart. They argue with the numbers in it.

A report built on handwritten notes and voicemail is a guess with gridlines. Three reasons the numbers do not hold up:

🫥

"Half the orders left no trace"

A call has no record, no stamp and no line items until somebody types one up. What was never captured cannot be measured.

🔤

"The same item, three spellings"

Typed in by three people over two years, so a report splits one product into three and the totals mean nothing.

🕰️

"We know the week, not the hour"

Without a timestamp you cannot tell an early order from a panic one, and that difference is the whole story.

Why Teams Compare

Why teams look for an alternative to Choco

Choco calls itself an AI platform for food and beverage wholesalers. It digitizes inbound orders, runs an online catalog that recommends items, and carries a sales hub and a foodservice CRM. None of that is a flaw. It is just a bigger purchase than some buyers want, and here is where a Choco competitor gets a second look.

📈

You came for order taking, not a growth engine

Choco leads on selling more: a catalog that suggests extra lines, rep tooling, bigger baskets. Worth paying for if growth is the project. If the actual complaint is that two people spend every morning on the phone, most of that spend sits idle.

📐

Built for everyone is not built for you

Choco says it wants to serve distributors of every size. We went the other way and picked one end of that range. Small and mid-size independents and DSD operations are the only buyer VOS is shaped around.

🖊️

A parsed message still gets read by you

Choco's OrderAgent lifts an order out of a voicemail, a text, even a photo of a handwritten pad, which is a genuinely clever bit of engineering. The catch is the same either way: the person who wrote that note did not see the lines your desk ended up with. Choco does have a free app its restaurant customers order in; this is about the messages that arrive instead.

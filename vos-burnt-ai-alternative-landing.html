Why Teams Compare

Why teams want an alternative to Burnt AI

Burnt calls itself an AI watchtower for food distribution and deploys agents by team: one for order intake, one for purchasing, one that rings customers about money they owe. It is a serious piece of engineering. It is also a lot to take on, and that is where a Burnt AI competitor gets looked at.

🤖

Software deciding, rather than your buyer

Burnt's own page says confidence thresholds decide when a human needs to look. That is a sensible design, and it still means the judgement call sits with the software and the exceptions land on your desk. Nowhere does it say the customer sees the order first.

🧰

Three agents when you wanted one habit

Intake, procurement, credit control. Each is a workflow to map, configure and trust. If the thing actually hurting you is the morning spent replaying voicemails, you are buying a program to solve a routine.

🚪

Someone has to come to you

Their onboarding is white-glove and, by their own description, their team works on your premises to understand the operation and set the agents up. A lot of distributors would rather not host a project at all.
